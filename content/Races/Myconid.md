# Description
The physical appearance of myconid is even more diverse than their flesh-and-blood counterparts. They have evolved appendages resembling arms and legs, as well as organs for taking in sensory information. Beyond that, they can be as different from one another as surface fungi are from each other, even within the same clan. Some myconid are tall and thin with caps like toadstools, while others might be short and squat like puffballs. Their diversity strengthens the survivability of their race and their clans.

Myconid have no gender, and they are capable of both sexual and asexual reproduction. Roughly once a year, a myconid is capable of creating a copy of itself via a specialized spore, which takes about 5 years to become an adult myconid. It’s also possible for two myconid to combine spores and create a completely new type of myconid. This process takes longer, and usually only takes place when the clan is in a settlement phase. An adult grows from this process after about 10 years, but such individuals are considered hardier and a good omen for a clan.

# Brief History
Myconid move through the deepest caverns of Eldaris in nomadic clans in search of nutrition. When they find pockets of nutrition, they may settle for a time, but eventually the nutrition runs dry and they move on. Nutrition to a myconid means the dead and decaying, only from decay can they gain life. Their appetites, while gruesome, counter the spread of disease beneath the earth. However, when the food runs dry, a desperate clan is not above causing death and decay.

It was the the dwarves who first made contact with the myconid, and in turn introduced them to the Union. The dwarves initially utilised the myconids purely for waste disposal in their cities. However, as time passed, the myconids were allowed more freedom. Several obedient clans were selected, and uplifted. They now serve the Union by consuming the rot it disgorges.

# Rarity in the Union
Myconid are rare, though a few clans have made permanent residence in the Union.

# Political Standing in the Union
The Union currently regards the myconid as a useful tool for waste disposal. When battles leave death and decay, or when a plague sweeps across the land, a myconid clan can restore the balance.

# Reputation in the Union
The myconid appetite for death and decay gives them a dark image in the eyes of the people, however few will dispute the usefulness of the clans.
