# Description
A typical goblin stands between 3.5 and 5 feet tall, with a slender, elongated build. Goblins' arms are unusually long and spindly, making them adept at climbing cliffs and trees. Their skin has a stony texture, ranging in colour from red-brown to moss green or grey. Their ears are large and swept back, their eyes are intensely red, and many sport heavy bone protrusions on their spines or elbows. Males have similar growths jutting from their chins, while females have heavier growths on their foreheads.

# Brief History
There are many theories behind why [[Ruin]] made the goblins, such a weak race did not match the god of war. The prevailing theory comes from the idea that war is more than simply overpowering your enemy. [[Ruin]] deliberately made the goblins weak, and made sure they knew they were weak. Forcing them from the very start to think differently about problems they faced. Goblins learnt the strength of guerrilla warfare, the value of traps and planning, perhaps most importantly the power of running away. 

The time of [[Ruin]] has long since passed for goblins however, they now find their own way in the world. Many stuck with the hobgoblins, some tried to make it on their own, and few joined the Union. In the union you can find goblins in almost any non leadership role. Their problem solving mindset, and low standards for happiness, made them ideal employees for owners wanting to cut costs. 

# Rarity in the Union
Goblins are common in the union, and are multiplying quickly.

# Political Standing in the Union
Goblins are more a subject of politics rather than a contributor to it.

# Reputation in the Union
Goblins in cities are generally seen as an unclean underclass, in rural areas goblins are likely still feared.
