# Description
Goliaths are towering, powerfully built humanoids, standing between 7 to 8 feet tall, with massive, well-defined muscles that speak of immense strength and endurance. Their ashen-grey skin, mottled with dark, naturally occurring markings, gives them a weathered, almost stone-like appearance, as if they were carved from the mountains themselves. Their chiselled features, bald heads, and piercing, often pale eyes contribute to their imposing presence, while their thick, calloused hands hint at a life of hardship and competition. Despite their size, goliaths move with careful, deliberate motions, their every step measured and efficient, reflecting their survivalist mentality.

# Brief History
The goliath were created by [[Rhodon]] as guardians of the Bitter Peaks. Much like the mountains that they called home, the early goliaths were hard and unforgiving. As far as they were concerned the Bitter Peaks were their sacred home, and outsiders were not welcome. So when early in the third era the goliaths left the Bitter Peaks in droves, the union prepared for war. Yet war was not on the goliaths agenda, they were on the run.

They threw themselves upon the mercy of the council, and begged for sanctuary. The goliaths of that generation were all haunted, broken by what made them flee. When asked about it the goliaths refused to speak. They feared that uttering it's name would draw it down from the Bitter Peaks, and bring death to all it found. The union eventually agreed to assimilate the goliaths, on the condition that their population be spread throughout the empire, and that their warriors join the Sect of Defence. In modern Eldaris military service is expected, but not required, and what drove the goliaths from their home has faded from memory.

# Rarity in the Union
Goliaths are uncommon in the union, however the population is spread far and wide. It's easy for goliaths to find work in the Sect of Defence, or any physically demanding job.

# Political Standing in the Union
Goliaths have little political sway, however those that serve in the Sect of Defence are favoured.

# Reputation in the Union
Goliaths are intimidating to most denizens of the empire, due to their impressive stature. Longer lived citizens, that remember the first goliath generation, are more scared of what a race so imposing would run from.
