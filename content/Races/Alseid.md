# Description
Alseid are elegant, deer-like humanoids, blending the grace of elves with the swiftness and agility of the wilds. Their upper bodies are slender and fey-like, with pointed ears, delicate features, and hair that often resembles the colours of the forest, rich browns, deep greens, or golden hues. Their lower bodies resemble that of a deer, complete with powerful, cloven-hoofed legs built for speed and endurance. Their short fur coats range from dappled white to deep chestnut, sometimes marked with the natural patterns of their woodland homes. Alseid eyes are wide and luminous, holding an unsettling depth, as if they see far beyond the mortal realm. Moving with an unearthly quiet, they embody the mystique of the deep woods, their presence both haunting and enchanting to those who encounter them.

# Brief History
The Alseid are a relatively new race on Eldaris, but that does not mean they are a young race, just one that has recently arrived. They claim to have been wandering the feywild for millennia, though the denizens of the wilds will attest that they only showed up a decade ago. For the alseid a once empty feywild is now populated with many wonderous people. The revelation that there are even more people and sights on other planes is the main driving force for alseid finding their way to the Union.

# Rarity in the Union
Alseid are exceedingly rare in the union. The population that appeared in the wilds was not large, and it is only a small percentage of them left the feywild.

# Political Standing in the Union
Alseid have no political power in the Union, they are barely recognised by it.

# Reputation in the Union
Alseid benefit from the possitve reputation of the centaur, despite having nothing to do with them.
