# Description
Axirians are lithe, amphibious humanoids with smooth, slick skin that ranges in hue from pale pinks and purples to deep aquatic blues and vibrant corals. Their most striking feature is the crown of feathery external gills that fan out from either side of their heads, pulsing gently with each breath. Large lidless eyes, often black or iridescent, give them an unblinking, almost otherworldly gaze, and their wide, expressive mouths lend a permanent air of calm curiosity. Their limbs are long and flexible, ending in webbed fingers and toes that allow them to move gracefully both in water and on land. Despite their delicate appearance, Axirians are resilient and serene, with an innate connection to the natural rhythms of the world around them.

# Brief History
The axirians have incredible regenerative biology, and natural healing talents, meaning they can shrug off almost any wound. Despite this, the axirians are dying out, and no one knows why. Elder axirians seem content to let their race fade into history, the young have no such compunction. Most axirians who leave their home are young adventurers on a lifelong quest to save their people.

# Rarity in the Union
The axirians are rare in the Union, and are getting rarer with each passing year. Unless the axirians find a way to survive, soon none will be seen anywhere in Eldaris.

# Political Standing in the Union
The axirians have no political power. However, they are considered by the Union to be a potential tool. If the Union could save the axirians in exchange for service, they could have an army of nigh on unkillable warriors at their command.

# Reputation in the Union
The axirians are generally looked upon with pity. Most races have to contend with mortality, but few have the weight of potentially being the last of their kind.
