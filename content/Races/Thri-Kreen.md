# Description
Thri-kreen are insectoid humanoids, their chitinous exoskeletons ranging in hues from sandy beige to deep emerald, blending seamlessly with arid landscapes. Towering over most humanoids, they move with an eerie, alien grace, their four arms shifting in perfect coordination. Their large, multifaceted eyes gleam with an otherworldly intelligence, unreadable to those unfamiliar with their kind. A pair of slender antennae twitch at the faintest disturbances, sensing vibrations in the air, while their powerful mandibles click softly when they communicate in their natural, chittering language. With elongated, digitigrade legs built for swift, silent movement, a thri-kreen can cross vast distances with unsettling ease, their predatory stillness and sudden bursts of speed marking them as both efficient hunters and enigmatic wanderers of the wilds.

# Brief History
In truth, very little is known of the thri-kreen. They are redescents of the Shattered Isles, but are thought to have originated somewhere other than Eldaris. They are a race disconnected from the world in many ways, they are reclusive, inscrutable, and possess an alien biology that appears insectoid but in truth has no relation to the insects of Eldaris. For the most part they appear to be willing to remain unseen by the world, and the powers that be are happy to let them do so.

# Rarity in the Union
Thri-kreen exceedingly rare in the Union. If any have travelled there, it might only be a handful.

# Political Standing in the Union
The thri-kreen are almost unnoticed by the politics of the Union.

# Reputation in the Union
