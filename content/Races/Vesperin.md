# Description
Vesperin are tall, four-armed humanoids with distinct insectoid features, most notably their large, heavy wings that drape around them like cloaks. Their faces are wholly inhuman, with delicate antennae and multifaceted, compound eyes that give them an unsettling yet captivating presence. Their bodies range from lightly furred to densely covered, adding to their ethereal and alien aesthetic. Their three-fingered hands and slender limbs give them a graceful, almost weightless movement, despite their imposing height.

# Brief History
The Union’s first whispers of the vesperin came not from scholars or explorers, but from sailors, half-mad from weeks at sea, mumbling tales of towering, winged figures watching them from the jungle canopy. Early expeditions into the Shattered Isles returned with only fragments: strange clicking echoes in the night, footprints too large for any known insect, and shadowy forms vanishing into the mist. A handful of traders swore they had glimpsed something unnatural in the trees, but their stories varied wildly, some claimed to see hulking beasts with too many arms, others described ghostly beings wrapped in shadowy cloaks.

Determined to uncover the truth, the Union dispatched scouts to follow the rumours, but every effort ended in silence and dead ends. The few supposed vesperin that have been encountered, only deepened the mystery. They spoke little, if at all, and vanished before any proper study could be conducted. In time, Union officials dismissed the notion of an entire civilization, instead classifying the vesperin as isolated anomalies, at best a few rare individuals with no greater society to uncover.

# Rarity in the Union
