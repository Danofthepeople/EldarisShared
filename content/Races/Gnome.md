# Description
Gnomes are small, sprightly humanoids, standing between 3 to 4 feet tall, with compact, nimble frames full of energy and curiosity. Their faces are round and expressive, with bright, inquisitive eyes that seem to sparkle with mischief and intelligence. Their skin ranges from fair to earthy tones, often paired with rosy cheeks, while their hair comes in a wild variety of colours, from natural browns and reds to striking silvers and even blues. Despite their size, gnomes are remarkably hardy, moving with a quick, purposeful energy that reflects their boundless enthusiasm for life. Whether deep in thought or lost in laughter, a gnome always seems on the verge of discovery or adventure, their restless nature driving them to explore, invent, and tinker with the world around them.

# Brief History
During the first long night, the dwarves dug deeper and deeper, trying to escape Zuzarlra's fiends. When all was over they did not emerge immediately, fearing that moving too soon would be the end of them. They were only coaxed out of their holes when they heard the sounds of rebuilding. They found their homes and settlements to be full of gnomes, diligently fixing the damage caused during the long night. The gnomes claimed that Xemos found them on a ship, and sent them to help. Not wanting to alienate their miracle helpers, the dwarves welcomed the gnomes with open arms. For many years gnomes lived almost exclusively with the dwarves, but as the union grew the gnomes spread across it. Each looking for a place to flex their mental acuity, and make the world a better place. Many of the technological advances of the union have come from gnomish minds. This makes them greatly respected across all races. Dwarves have a special kinship with the gnomes to this day, for pulling them out of the darkness all those years ago.

# Rarity in the Union
Gnomes are somewhat common in the union. They are usually found in any field that can benefit from a bit of brainpower, though gnomes are sometimes picky on what they'll turn their minds to.

# Political Standing in the Union
Gnomes care very little for the politics of the union, however they are favoured by it for the most part.

# Reputation in the Union
Gnomes for the most part are seen as well meaning folk, a little eccentric but harmless.
