# Description
Centaurs are majestic, powerful beings, their upper bodies resembling strong, well-built humanoids, while their lower halves take the form of a graceful, muscular equine frame. Standing between 6 to 7 feet tall, with their full length reaching nearly 9 feet, they possess broad shoulders, toned arms, and a commanding presence. Their skin tones vary widely, often matching their horse-like lower halves, which come in a range of coat colors and patterns. Their manes and tails are thick and flowing, sometimes adorned with braids or beads that reflect their culture and status. Centaurs move with natural grace and speed, their powerful legs making them swift and tireless travellers, equally adept at thundering into battle or galloping through open plains.

# Brief History
Of the fey touched races, the centaur have been present on Eldaris the longest. It was early in the first era of The Divine Council when the centaur first emerged. At this time there were no known crossing points between the material plane and the feywild, additionally planar theory was still in it's infancy. Thus the herd that made the crossing did so without noticing, only realising that something had changed when the sky shifted away from twilight. This herd of the lost walked Eldaris cautiously for many years, seeing no signs of intelligent life, and finding no path back home.

Their travels brought them across the young union. The herd saw the denizens of the union struggling in the wilds, Eldaris doing it's best to prevent civilisation taking root. The herd master made a choice that changed the path of their people. No more would the centaur wander, searching for a home long since lost. They would make a home of this new world, and protect those within it. Centaur became the first rangers, keepers of the balance between civilisation and nature. Thousands of years later the herd of the lost is gone, and it's descendants have a home in the union.

# Rarity in the Union
Centaur are uncommon in the union. They are more common in border settlements which need more protection, but can be found anywhere.

# Political Standing in the Union
Centaur are politically respected in the union, their power comes from their aid in the union's expansion efforts.

# Reputation in the Union
Centaur are respected by most in the union, people tend to feel safer around them.
