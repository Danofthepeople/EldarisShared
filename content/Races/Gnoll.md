# Description
Gnolls are tall, hyena-like humanoids with lanky yet powerful frames built for speed and ferocity. Their coarse fur ranges from dusty yellows to deep browns, often mottled with spots or stripes, and their muzzles are filled with jagged, predatory teeth capable of crushing bone. Their long limbs end in clawed hands and feet, giving them a loping, almost beast-like gait, while their large, expressive ears twitch at every sound. Though often hunched, a gnoll standing at full height cuts an imposing figure, a perfect blend of savage instinct and terrifying cunning.

# Brief History
In the Shattered Isles gnolls are seen as uncivilised brutes, barely one step above the animals they resemble. Whilst the other races progressed in response to the arrival of the Union, the gnolls if anything regressed. More and more they became bullies, set on taking what they could not build themselves. The other races of the isles and the Union do not tolerate them willingly, gnolls are simply too tenacious to die.

# Rarity in the Union
Gnolls are exceedingly rare in the Union, few are the gnolls who can walk in Union society while following its rules and customs.

# Political Standing in the Union
Gnolls are a subject of politics, and not a good one.

# Reputation in the Union
Most gnolls are as uncivilised as they are made out to be, this makes life for the exceptions very difficult.
