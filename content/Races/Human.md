# Description
Humans are versatile, medium-sized humanoids, typically standing between 5 to 6.5 feet tall with varied builds ranging from lean to muscular. Their skin tones, hair colors, and facial features vary greatly depending on their ancestry, ranging from pale to deep brown skin, and hair from white-blond to black, straight or curled. They have flat faces, expressive eyes, and highly adaptable physiques, allowing them to thrive in nearly any environment. Unlike many other races, humans lack innate physical advantages, but their resilience, endurance, and adaptability make them capable of excelling in a wide range of skills and professions. Their movements are quick and efficient, lacking the raw power of some races but making up for it with ingenuity, determination, and sheer willpower.

# Brief History
Humans were born from the weave of Eldaris, spun into being at the end of the forging by Xemos. It is through this connection to the weave that humans are connected with the world around them. It provides them an extraordinary ability to adapt to their circumstances, learn new skills and survive against any odds. Humanity was slow to progress however, in the first contact war they were outclassed in both technology and tactics. With sheer force of numbers they managed to cling on, adapting, stealing and learning, becoming just as much a threat as the dwarves and orcs.   After the first contact war humanity began to spread. Multiplying at a rate that outstripped the other races. It was not long before humans became a wide reaching scattered people, with hundreds of ideologies and ways of life. Humanity was so vast and diverse that groups began to fight each other, every group determined to cement their way of living as the right way. Humans continued their self warring ways all the way up to the first long night. Ironically humanity's obsession with self destruction had trained them well, though fiends still massacred thousands the humans were able to hold the line, and in the milennia that have followed humanity has remained unbroken.

# Rarity in the Union
Humans are ubiquitous in the Union, they are the most common race in Eldaris.

# Political Standing in the Union
Humans wield almost unrivalled political power in the union, due to their ubiquitousness and due to the number of human primordial aspects.

# Reputation in the Union
Humans are treated well in the Union, as most in the Union are humans. The other races often hold disdain for humans, though they would rarely express it openly.
