# Description
Neither bugs nor bears, bugbears are the hulking cousins of goblins and hobgoblins. They are long of limb and covered in coarse hair, with wedge-shaped ears and pointed teeth. Despite their formidable build, bugbears capable of hiding in spaces seemingly too small for them. This affinity for stealth coupled with their brute strength makes them deadly ambushers.

# Brief History
[[Ruin]] created the bugbears as shock troops, as such bugbears excel at approaching their enemies unnoticed then striking with overwhelming force. Though [[Ruin]] long since lost control of the bugbears, most would rather steal than sow. Many live a life of laziness, only occasionally stirring to bully and steal from Union citizens. 

There are of course exceptions, one is an aspect of Valia, and within hobgoblin territories bugbears are thought to be vital members of society. Even within the Union some bugbears have found a survivable niche. Most end up as toughs, protecting legitimate businesses, or working for crime lords. Some use their powerful builds for heavy labour, making an honest living. Over time it's thought bugbears temperaments will mellow to a point where they can fill any societal role, until then most other races keep an eye on the bugbears, just in case.

# Rarity in the Union
Bugbears are rare in the union. Those that are present usually hold positions of manual labour, or tend towards violent careers.

# Political Standing in the Union
Bugbears, in general, barely understand the politics of the union, let alone contribute to it.

# Reputation in the Union
Bugbears are feared by most denizens of the union, especially those that don't live in large cities.
