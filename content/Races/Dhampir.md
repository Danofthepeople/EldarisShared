# Description
Dhamphir bear many of the physical traits of their original race, though their half vampiric nature is obvious to anyone who cares to look. Unatural eyes, deathly pallor, sharp teeth, all are dead giveaways of a dhampir. They are fast, they can run on walls, the dark is no barrier to them, they have no need to breathe, and they have the hunger. What they do with these gifts, whether they can resist the hunger, that depends on the individual.

# Brief History
Dhampir are a relatively new occurence on Eldaris. Roughly one undred and fifty years ago, the Faited Few ventured into the plane of blood to slay hunger itself. The living curse that drove all vampires to consume blood. The Faited Few slew that curse, and the thirteen original vampires took its place. 

Vampirisim was forever changed, blood was no longer the only thing a vampire could feed upon. Each of the thirteen offered a different option, in addition to blood. Neither the Thirteen or the Faited Few knew the full consequences of this. 

One such consequence was the lessening of Azrus's ire towards vampires. Though the sun god still hates vampire kind, life is no longer rigidly separated from vampirisim. The two can intermingle, connecting life and undeath. When this happens, a dhampir is created.

# Rarity in the Union
Dhampir are rare in the union, and are generally concentrated in lands controlled by Ginmik Bronzearm.

# Political Standing in the Union
Dhampir have high standing in the lands of Ginmik Bronzearm, this politcal standing diminshes the further a dhampir gets from his lands.

# Reputation in the Union
The repuation of dhampir is heavily influenced by the veputation of vampires. Most will see dhampir as a threat, but a few relate them with monster hunting heroes.
