# Description
Loxodon are massive, elephantine humanoids, their towering frames covered in thick, leathery skin that ranges from ashen grey to dusty brown. Their most distinctive features are their long, dexterous trunks, which they use with remarkable precision, and their broad, tusked faces, which carry an air of both wisdom and quiet strength. Every step they take is deliberate and measured, their immense weight making their movements seem slow but never clumsy. Their large, expressive eyes and gently flaring ears give them an aura of patience and deep contemplation, yet when roused, they can unleash unstoppable force, their sheer size and power making them formidable both in battle and in presence. Draped in ornate robes or simple wrappings, many loxodons carry an air of stoic dignity, their very existence a testament to endurance, tradition, and the strength found in unwavering resolve.

# Brief History
When the union tried to settle the island, that would later be known as The White Tusk, they ran into unusual difficulties. The first was simply approaching the island, they found that the wind consistently seemed to block their efforts at advancing. Not to be deterred the prospective colonists set to rowing to shore. This left them exhausted when they arrived, a quick camp was set up and the colonists slept. When they awoke they found themselves back out at sea, with all their belongings.

Confused and tired the colonists took time to plan. The next time they got to the island they posted guards, they still woke up back at sea their guards remembering nothing. By this point half the colonists were set on leaving, the other half determined to tame the island. Unable to reach consensus the colonists split into two groups, one rowed to the island with half the supplies, and sent the row boats back to the second group. The second group left to scout for other promising islands. 

After spending three consecutive nights undisturbed on The White Tusk, the colonists began building. Little did they know they were being watched by disapproving eyes. On the seventh day the loxodon emerged. Insisting that if these people were to stay on their island home, that they at least build somewhere that wouldn't be flooded in the next rainfall.

# Rarity in the Union
Loxodon are rare in the union, however their population is slowly growing and spreading throughout the union.

# Political Standing in the Union
Loxodon have little political power.

# Reputation in the Union
Loxodon are seen as kindly giants, with high standards. Hard to impress, but always willing to help.
