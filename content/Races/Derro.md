# Description
The derro are stunted, dwarf like humanoids, small of stature with wide shoulders and stubby limbs. They stand between 3 and 4 feet tall, with males only slightly taller than females on average. Their slender builds rarely weigh more than 40 pounds. Their pale skin is translucent, revealing a ghostly web of blue veins. Their large, pure white staring eyes gleam in the dark. Derro are quick and erratic in their movements. Their elaborately braided or styled hair ranges from white to pale straw in colour. Males take great pride in their prodigious moustaches, which are often braided and waxed.

# Brief History
Little is known about the derro, other than the fact they are insane. It is thought they are not born this way, but instead are driven to insanity by their kin. Perhaps insanity is required to live in the deep subterranean places the derro call home. Perhaps they know something that insanity can shield them from. Perhaps somewhere in the dark, there is a mad god twisting the minds of these creatures towards insanity, generation after generation.

# Rarity in the Union
The derro are exceedingly rare in the Union, at least on the surface.

# Political Standing in the Union
The derro have no political standing in the Union, and their insanity has led the Union to disregard them.

# Reputation in the Union
Most will not have heard of the derro, and those who have may dismiss their existence as a folk tale. Should a citizen of the Union meet a derro however, they would surely recognise the derro's insanity and flee.
