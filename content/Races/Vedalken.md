# Description
Vedalken are tall, slender humanoids, standing between 6 to 7 feet tall, with lithe, elongated frames built more for intellect than physical power. Their smooth, hairless skin ranges in shades of blue, from pale cerulean to deep navy, often giving them an otherworldly appearance. Their faces are sharp and refined, with high cheekbones, narrow features, and large, thoughtful eyes that shimmer with keen intelligence. Lacking external ears, their hearing is finely tuned, and their long, dexterous fingers make them naturally adept at intricate work, from crafting to spellcasting.

# Brief History
When [[Xarxes]] was born so were the vedalken. Unlike the rest of the prime races the vedalken were isolated. They lived within the avatar of [[Xarxes]], within the impossible citadel. Much like the god they lived within, they were stuck. Straying more than a few miles from the citadels protection meant a cold death. This lead the vedalken to look inward, within their own minds and the halls of [[Xarxes]]. The vedalken were gifted with a natural propensity for learning. They quickly grasped the mundane, and learnt the arcane at an astounding rate. By the time of the first contact war vedalken magic had advanced greatly. Though the vedalken were still trapped, they were able to watch the war from afar.  

After the first contact war the vedalken redoubled their efforts to find a way to leave the citadel. They yearned to meet the other races, to learn from them and to share their own knowledge. Despite their efforts the vedalken couldn't find escape, all their efforts produced spectacular and deadly failures. When the first long night fell the vedalken were largely unaffected. No gates to hell opened around [[Xarxes]]. In fact the only reason they knew something was wrong was due to darkness and their observation of the other races. Not being able to aid the other races only made the vedalken feel helpless. For five days they watched the slaughter, many vedalken sacrificed themselves trying to escape [[Xarxes]], to use their magic to provide protection. On the dawn of the sixth day, one succeeded, the first vedalken divine.  

With the gates open vedalken left [[Xarxes]] in droves. Each excited to join the union, meet new races and make new friends. Though welcomed for their magical skill, vedalken were seen as overbearing, and ultimately did not integrate for long. Vedalken tried to correct this behaviour, returning to [[Xarxes]] and preparing themselves to be a part of something greater. When the vedalken reopened the gates to [[Xarxes]] late in the second era of The Divine Empire, they returned as a colder race, their emotions masked. This time they were accepted.

# Rarity in the Union
Vedalken are rare the union, and their once large civilisation in [[Xarxes]] has disappeared along with the god.

# Political Standing in the Union
The vedalken used to have a lot of political sway in the Union.  However, with their only divine slumbering like the rest, and no primordial aspects, the vedalken have lost almost all of their political power.

# Reputation in the Union
Vedalken are respected, they seem cold to the other races, but most know once a vedalken opens up they feel just as much as anyone else.
