# Description
The satarre are a tall, unnervingly alien people, their smooth, pallid skin stretched taut over elongated, sinewy frames. Their featureless, lidless eyes glisten with an eerie awareness, betraying emotions that are difficult to read. A crest of chitinous spines rises from the backs of their heads, shifting subtly with their moods, while their mouths, lined with rows of needled teeth, curl into expressions that seem almost, but not quite, familiar. Their three-jointed fingers, tipped with fine, black claws, move with an unsettling precision, and their long, powerful tails sway with calculated grace. Draped in intricate, layered garb, often reinforced with organic plating, the satarre give the impression of something half-remembered from a fever dream, unmistakably intelligent, yet fundamentally apart from the world as others know it.

# Brief History
The satarre linger on the edges of reality, their existence whispered in fearful hushes among sailors and explorers of the Shattered Isles. Fleeting glimpses of their tall, alien forms vanish between the trees, their black eyes reflecting an understanding too deep, too wrong. Those who claim to have seen them speak in fevered tones of figures that do not walk, but arrive, slipping into sight as though stepping from another world, only to fade just as easily. The Union has no official record of their kind, only the scattered journals of doomed men who took their own lives shortly after their encounters. Whatever the satarre are, whatever they want, remains unknowable.

# Rarity in the Union
The satarre are near mythical. If one exists in the union, it might be the only one.

# Political Standing in the Union
Myths do not have political sway.

# Reputation in the Union
The vast majority of people do not think the satarre exist, and that is if they have even heard of them.
