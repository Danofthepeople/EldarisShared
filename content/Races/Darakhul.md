# Description
Risen darakhul start out resembling their original race. However, most of a darakhul’s cosmetic features, such as supple skin or a long beard, fade over time into the gaunt, skull-like face, fanged maw, and taut, grey skin of a ghoulish undead. 

# Brief History
There is an insidious infection on Eldaris. When an infected individual dies, they rise as a ghoul. An undead creature with an almost mindless hunger for flesh. Those could be considered the lucky ones, for they lack the ability to be repulsed by what they do. 

Some are not stripped of their personality, these we call darakhul. They are remade to be predators of sapient life, with an endless hunger that drives them to kill and consume. This curse breaks most who bear it, their personalities are reforged into something much darker. Some few refuse the hunger, and starve. Fewer still can accept it without losing themself.

# Rarity in the Union
Darakhul are very rare in the Union.

# Political Standing in the Union
Darakhul are considered a threat to the population, and are treated with hostility.

# Reputation in the Union
Unlike the unkind rumours about other races, darakhul will actually eat your children. As such they are almost universally feared and hated.
