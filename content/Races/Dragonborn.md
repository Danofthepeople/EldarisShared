# Description
Dragonborn are tall, imposing reptilian humanoids, standing between 6 to 7.5 feet tall, with broad, muscular frames built for power and endurance. Their scaly hides come in a variety of colors—red, blue, green, black, white, gold, silver, and more—often reflecting their draconic ancestry. They have strong, angular snouts, piercing reptilian eyes, and ridged or crested heads that give them a regal, draconic appearance. Unlike true dragons, they lack wings and tails, but their powerful claws and sharp teeth make them formidable in battle. Many have a natural presence that commands attention, and their movements are purposeful and confident, reflecting the pride, discipline, and inner fire that defines their kind.

# Brief History
When Udremm ascended to godhood he scattered eggs across Teitsa'Var. These eggs bore the first dragonborn, Udremm's image of the perfect humanoid race. The dragonborn were elevated to greatness within Teitsa'Var, with each clan ruling vast swathes of land. The only authority higher than them, was the dragons themselves. Like dragons, the clans were very territorial. They quickly ended up joining in the civil wars that are so common in Teitsa'Var. Some clans follow ancient dragons, others forge their own path. The only commonality between the clans is their greed, their lust for power and control. No clan is strong enough to claim the land, and the dragon gods are content to pit them against each other for their own machinations. This has driven the clans to look outwards. To find allies from different soil. This in turn lead each clan to Union, each sending ambassadors to secure the Union's aid. Over the past few centuries more and more came to the Union, some bringing the conflict with them, others wanting to escape it. Regardless the civil war goes on, with no sign of stopping.

# Rarity in the Union
Dragonborn are rare in the union, with most sticking to the large cities.

# Political Standing in the Union
Dragonborn have a good level of political power in the empire, as they benefit from the respect given to the dragons, and their gods Udremm and Imrag.

# Reputation in the Union
Dragonborn, to most in the union, evoke the awe, and fear, of a true dragon.
