# Description
Raven-like humanoids with sharp beaks, claws and no wings. The corvians resemble kenku to an uncanny degree. Only the expert eye, and ear, can tell them apart. Unlike kenku, corvians are capable of both speech and creative thought, though they are also capable of mimicry that is almost on par with kenku.

# Brief History
Corvians are believed to be a malicious creation of one of the darker gods, though there is no proof of their origins. There is no record of how long they have existed for. 

The Union might never have discovered them if not for a few of their agents being exposed. They had been impersonating kenku, harvesting Union secrets, and were caught selling them to foreign powers. These few captured individuals revealed almost nothing about their kind, other than the fact they were born and trained somewhere in the Shattered Isles

# Rarity in the Union
It is impossible to accurately count the number of corvians in the Union, in theory any kenku could be one of them.

# Political Standing in the Union
Corvians are officially enemies of state, and will be arrested if found.

# Reputation in the Union
Few know the corvians exist, those few hate them.

# Mechanics
