# Description
Elves are tall, graceful humanoids, standing between 5.5 to 6.5 feet tall, with slender, agile builds that seem almost ethereal. Their skin tones vary widely, from deep earth tones to pale, almost luminous hues, often reflecting their ancestral connection to nature or magic. Their faces are finely sculpted, with high cheekbones, angular features, and piercing, almond-shaped eyes that gleam with intelligence and ancient wisdom. Their ears are long and pointed, a hallmark of their kind, and their hair flows in silken strands, often in shades of gold, silver, black, or deep autumnal reds. Elves move with unearthly poise and quiet precision, their steps light and deliberate, as if they barely disturb the world around them. There is a sense of ageless mystery in their presence, their expressions often serene yet enigmatic, hinting at the deep wells of knowledge and experience they carry from their long-lived existence.

# Brief History
Elves in Eldaris have only been around since the start of the third era. In fact they appeared across Eldaris on the very first day of the era. Their divine brought them from the feywild, and demanded a place on the new council. After the events of the second era the council was bound to accept, thus the tenth seat went to the elves. Beyond their bold entrance and well timed demands however, the elves seemed lost. They had no memory of their time in the feywild, but were almost certain that they were kicked out. Their memory also wasn't completely blank. They could remember their identity, skills, language, family and friends. Elves generally accept that what remained of their minds was all that was important. 

Where other races may leave their legacy in what they build, an elf's legacy is the perfection of self. There are no elven cities, for to an elf home comes from within. This leads most elves to adopt a nomadic lifestyle, travelling from place to place, adopting new skills and meeting new friends. Some travel in family groups, others alone and some with friends. An elf only sticks around for as long as it takes them to experience they desire, and learn all they can.

# Rarity in the Union
Elves are uncommon in the union, however they can be found almost anywhere doing almost anything.

# Political Standing in the Union
Elves could be politically powerful in the union, since they have a few primordial aspects, however their nomadic nature divorces them from most politics.

# Reputation in the Union
Elves are misunderstood by most in the union, but are generally seen as friendly.
