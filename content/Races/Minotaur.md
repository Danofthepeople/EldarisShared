# Description
Minotaur are towering, broad-shouldered humanoids with the heads of powerful bulls, their forms built for raw strength and relentless endurance. Their thick, corded muscles are covered in short, coarse fur, ranging in colour from deep browns and blacks to sandy hues, while their large, curved horns serve as both a weapon and a mark of status. Their heavy hooves thunder against the ground with each step, and their deep-set eyes burn with an intensity that speaks of both instinctual fury and unwavering determination. Though many see them as nothing more than brutal warriors, their movements are precise and deliberate, honed by either years of training or the unyielding trials of survival. A minotaur's presence alone is often enough to command respect or fear, depending on whether they stand beside you or against you.

# Brief History
The minotaur originate from an island completely covered in twisting stone walls, fittingly named Labyrinthia. It was never seen as a potential colony target by the union, due to the large structure covering it's landmass. It did however attract the attention of adventurers and scholars alike. Early expeditions to the island focused on bypassing the maze, attempting to travel over the top of it, however all attempts were thwarted by the magic the maze itself. This only invigorated the passions of the expeditionary forces, convincing them that great treasure must be protected somewhere within. Yet most parties that entered the labyrinth lost their way, finding themselves ejected from the maze. Others were less fortunate, never finding their way out. However the rare few who did find treasure, ensured that the expeditions never dried up. 

Durand Ashenforth, a scholar that devoted his whole life to the exploration of Labyrinthia, was the one to discover the minotaur that called the labyrinth their home. He did so on his last expedition, succumbing to old age he had decided that he would walk the maze one last time. This time without the goal of unlocking it's secrets, just with the desire to say goodbye. Months later his body was carried out of the maze by the minotaur. He had spent his dying days among them, teaching them of the world and finally unravelling one of Labyrinthia's secrets. To the minotaur Durand was a herald of the wider world, and they now explore it to honour him.

# Rarity in the Union
Minotaur are very rare in the union, most of their population still resides in Labyrinthia, but with each year more emerge.

# Political Standing in the Union
Minotaur have very little political power, but are in general fascinated by the politics of the union

# Reputation in the Union
Minotaur are met with the same fascination they radiate out to the world, though they may be intimidating, most folk love to hear of the mysterious island of Labyrinthia.
