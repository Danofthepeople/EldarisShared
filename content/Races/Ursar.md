# Description
The Ursar are towering, broad-shouldered humanoids with the unmistakable presence of a walking fortress. Their powerful limbs end in large, clawed hands, capable of crushing stone or wielding weapons with effortless might, yet their movements are measured and deliberate, betraying a deep sense of control and restraint.

Ursar are covered in dense fur, ranging in colour from deep black to rich brown, golden hues, or even stark white, depending on their lineage and homeland. Their fur grows thicker in colder climates, making them naturally resistant to the elements. Their broad, heavy-set faces bear short muzzles and small, watchful eyes that gleam with deep intelligence and patience, taking in their surroundings with calm precision.

# Brief History
The Union's first official introduction to the ursar was Kagranak, the black bear, an aspect of [[Ruin]]. Kagranak led armies against the union, killed a divine, and successfully conquered the Union's westernmost state Vygofeln.

The Union's true first introduction was decades before, when the Union dispatched their exalted to slaughter the ursar on their home island. Kagranak's ascension had been prophesised, and the Union hoped to kill Kagranak before he could ascend into [[Ruin|Ruin's]] service. Some believe that without this slaughter Kagranak would not have felt the need to wage war on the Union. That the prophecy was a ploy of [[Ruin]] to make certain a future where the Union would be in a losing war. Regardless of the truth, Kagranak ascended, and thousands died to sate his need for revenge.

# Rarity in the Union
The ursar are either exceedingly rare, or incredibly reclusive. The only well known ursar is Kagranak himself. Whilst some ursar were seen fighting under Kagranak's banner in the war, very few have been seen since.

# Political Standing in the Union
With Kagranak being an aspect of [[Ruin]], he can challenge the leaders of the Union on any matter. This grants the ursar a level of political protection, but their sway does not extend far beyond that.

# Reputation in the Union
Most union citizens only know of one ursar, Kagranak. Since Kagranak is well known for conquering portions of the Union, and being an aspect of war, most people in the Union assume all ursar are power hungry warmongers.
