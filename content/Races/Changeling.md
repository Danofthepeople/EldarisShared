# Description
In their true form, changelings appear faded, their features almost devoid of detail. It is rare to see a changeling in that form, for a typical changeling changes their shape the way others might change clothes. A casual shape, one created on the spur of the moment, with no depth or history, is called a mask. A mask can be used to express a mood or to serve a specific purpose and then might never be used again. However, many changelings develop identities that have more depth, crafting whole personas complete with histories and beliefs. A changeling adventurer might have personas for many situations, including negotiation, investigation, and combat.

# Brief History
The darkest tales speak of monsters that can steal your face. Creatures who want nothing more than to infiltrate society, replace your loved ones, and kill you in your sleep. The truth of changelings is a little different, but truth rarely changes perception. Nobody knows why some humans are born as changelings, and there are scant few theories on how they can manipulate their own weave to change forms at will. 

What is known, as with all shape changers, is silver is the only ward against them. Hand a changeling a silver coin, and it's hand will turn milky white. Changelings that wish to live an honest life in the union often pierce their bodies with silver, thus displaying their true form around the metal. For many, the constant discomfort a fine price for a chance at life, while others risk everything to hide their true self. In the union stealing another's form carries a brutal punishment. Rods of silver drilled into bone, so that the changeling may never take another form again.

# Rarity in the Union
Changlings are incredibly rare in the union, as there is no discernible pattern to their birth even keeping track of their population is difficult. Changelings are also infertile, and nobody knows why.

# Political Standing in the Union
Changelings have no political sway in the union. The only role they play is that of spies, at great risk to themselves.

# Reputation in the Union
Changelings are greatly shunned and feared in the union. The only way for them to have a somewhat normal life, is to either hide their nature as long as they live, or pierce their flesh with silver.

# Mechanics
The rules for this race can be found in the Roll20 compendium.

In addition, changelings in Eldaris have a weakness to silver. It causes them discomfort to touch, and suspends their ability to shapeshift in the localised area. Some changelings can train themselves to hold silver for short durations, but piercings make the silver irresistible.
