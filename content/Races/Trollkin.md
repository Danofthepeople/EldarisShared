# Description
With a thick hide of green or brown, trollkin are superficially similar to orcs or hobgoblins. While many trollkin share a rough and unkempt appearance, their inhuman lineage can show itself in a wide variety of forms. Some trollkin could even be considered beautiful by human standards, except for some subtle marker of their monstrosity, such as stone–grey skin or talon-tipped fingers.

Many trollkin wear tattoos to show tribal loyalty or to venerate their ancestor spirits. The bravest among them brand their flesh to show mastery over their own fear, since only acid and fire leave long-lasting scars on their skin.

# Brief History
Trollkin are the result of a union between a human and a troll. Unlike most unions, where the resulting child would share the mother's race, in a paring between a troll and a human a trollkin is all but guaranteed. Few know why this is the case, but the most popular theory is that the regenerative nature of trolls binds perfectly with the adaptable weave of humankind. What is stranger, the race is entirely stable and dominant. A trollkin who has a child with either a human or a troll is almost guaranteed to have a trollkin baby.

Trollkin are a young race on Eldaris, they have only existed for around one hundred and fifty years, leading many to believe their existence is related to the ascension of [[Hadren]], the god of giants.

# Rarity in the Union
Trollkin are very rare on Eldaris, though the dominate nature of their genes means that their population might surge if they integrate with humanity.

# Political Standing in the Union
Trollkin are a subject of politics, not a contributor to it.

# Reputation in the Union
Trollkin are currently seen as monsters, largely because few people can believe that a human would willingly sleep with a troll, leading to dark speculations about their conception.
