# Description
Hexbloods have most of the physical characteristics of their original race. However, there are always signs that tell a hexblood apart For example, ears that split in forked points, skin in lurid shades, long hair that regrows if cut, or an irremovable living crown. Along with these marks, hexbloods manifest hag-like traits, such as darkvision and a variety of magical methods to beguile the senses and avoid the same.

# Brief History
It is generally believed that hexbloods have existed for as long as mortals have been stupid enough to make deals with hags. In reality, the magic that can turn someone into a hexblood has existed since the dawn of Eldaris, hags merely do a better job of remembering the process. Still it is most likely that if you see a hexblood in the Union, at some point in their past they made an ill-advised deal with a hag.

# Rarity in the Union
Hexbloods are very rare in the Union, though they are becoming more common as people start looking to alternate means of gaining power.

# Political Standing in the Union
Hexbloods have a slightly lower political standing than members of their former race.

# Reputation in the Union
Hexbloods are often met with suspicion as people cannot help but wonder just how much they gave away for power.
