# Description
Death isn’t always the end. The reborn exemplify this, being individuals who have died yet, somehow, still live. Some reborn exhibit the scars of fatal ends, their ashen flesh or bloodless veins making it clear that they’ve been touched by death. Other reborn are marvels of magic or science, being stitched together from disparate beings or bearing mysterious minds in manufactured bodies. Whatever their origins, reborn know a new life and seek experiences and answers all their own.

# Brief History
The defiance of death is a natural pursuit of mortal kind. Within certain bounds, the gods accept and even encourage this pursuit. Over the millennia many forms of life after death have been discovered. Some are the result of natural phenomena, others the product of dark rituals, and others still the product of advanced artifice. Regardless of their origins, Eldaris knows them as the reborn.

# Rarity in the Union
It is hard to get an accurate count of the reborn, as some can blend perfectly with society, however they are believed to be very rare.

# Political Standing in the Union
The Union does not actively differentiate between a member of a race, and a reborn of the same race. This means that reborn roughly share the political standing of their original race.

# Reputation in the Union
The people of the Union are no stranger to corpses being reused, the soulless have been around for millennia after all. Therefore the reborn are generally treated as their original race would be, unless they have physical characteristics that are off putting to the average person.
