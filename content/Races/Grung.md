# Description
Grungs are small, amphibious humanoids resembling vibrant, poison-skinned tree frogs. Standing barely over two feet tall, their sleek, slippery bodies are covered in smooth, rubbery skin that comes in a variety of striking colours, each denoting their caste within grung society. Their wide, bulbous eyes gleam with an intense, almost unsettling focus, while their long, dexterous fingers and toes end in sharp pads that allow them to cling to surfaces with unnatural ease. Constantly moist and exuding toxic secretions, a grung’s very presence is a danger to the unwary. Despite their small size, their agile movements and sudden, darting leaps make them unpredictable, vanishing into the treetops just as quickly as they strike.

# Brief History
Grung society is a caste system. Each caste lays eggs in a separate hatching pool, and juvenile grungs join their caste upon emergence from the hatchery. All grungs are a dull greenish grey when they are born, but each individual takes on the colour of its caste as it grows to adulthood. From lowest to highest caste, grungs can be green, blue, purple, red, orange, or gold.

Green grungs are the tribe's warriors, hunters, and laborers, and blue grungs work as artisans and in other domestic roles. Supervising and guiding both groups are the purple grungs, which serve as administrators and commanders. Red grungs are the tribe's scholars and magic users. They are superior to purple, blue, and green grungs and given proper respect even by grungs of higher status. Higher castes include orange grungs, which are elite warriors that have authority over all lesser grungs, and gold grungs, which hold the highest leadership positions. A tribe's sovereign is always a gold grung.

Most grungs are lawful, having been raised in a strict caste system. They tend toward evil as well, coming from a culture where social advancement occurs rarely, and most often because another member of your army has died and there is no one else of that caste to fill the vacancy.

Despite this tendency to evil, their strict adherence to laws makes them useful to the other races of the Isles and the Union. When distasteful acts are required, grungs can always be hired for wealth, treasure or status.

# Rarity in the Union
Grungs are exceedingly rare in the Union. The state does not want to be host to a large population of grungs it has only allowed individuals to take residence.

# Political Standing in the Union
Grungs exist in the political grey area of being a useful too, but a distasteful ally.

# Reputation in the Union
Most people dislike the grung, their evil reputation is well known.
