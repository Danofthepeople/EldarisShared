# Description
Orcs are tall, powerfully built humanoids, standing between 6.5 to 8 feet tall, with dense muscle and a natural air of authority. Their skin ranges from deep gray to earthen green, often marked by faint natural ridges or lines that grow more defined with age, reflecting their long lifespans and the wisdom they accumulate. They have strong, chiseled features, prominent lower tusks, and piercing eyes that gleam with intelligence and determination. Unlike crude stereotypes, orcs carry themselves with graceful confidence, moving with measured strength and controlled purpose, as every action is a reflection of their personal merit.

# Brief History
The orcs of Eldaris have a long and troubled history. Before the first contact war they were a prosperous race. Due to their meritocratic nature, the best orc in any given field has total authority. Though disputes of skill are common, as soon as the superior orc is determined the rest are usually happy to follow their lead. This meant their early civilization developed dramatically, in size, culture, and technology. When the first contact war broke out the orcs had the advantage in both discipline and tactics. Following the war humans and dwarves recognised unique orcish mentality, and feared it. For if one orc was to prove themselves superior to all the rest, every orc would be united under them. As new races emerged they were taught of the threat orcs pose. Thus skirmishes, population control and extermination, became common occurrences to orcish kind. 

The orcs were not broken by this however, surviving and even thriving into the second era of The Divine Council. This was when one of their own ascended to become divine. Vygosh demanded his rightful place on The Divine Council, in response the council declared war. The Divine Union followed the council's command and began striking the orcs on every front, but united under one leader, the orcs held. While Vygosh lived no divine would face him for fear of death, and the stalemate remained unbroken. Just when it seemed Vygosh would turn the tide, the council unleashed the exalted. Though the exalted numbered only five, it was enough. Vygosh was slain, and the orcish resistance crumbled.  

Unsatisfied with merely beating the orcs, The Council pushed for eradication. Without their divine to unite them the orcs were systematically crushed by the council. Facing extinction, an unknown war chief made a deal with Tadon the god of storms. Protection, a chance at survival, at the cost of carrying Tadon's rage. In modern Eldaris orcish connection with Tadon has faded. They still have some minor favour with the god, and on occasion still feel his rage, but with each passing year the effect is lessened. The deal with Tadon allowed the orcs time to find a new place in the world. They are few, and are spread far and wide in isolated tribes. Life as a modern orc isn't easy, many hate or fear the orcs still, especially in the Union that once sought to destroy them.

Orcs are rare in the union.

# Political Standing in the Union
Orcs have no real political power in the union.

# Reputation in the Union
Orcs are feared, not because they are savages, but because of their potential.
