# Description
Muscled and cartilaginous terrors, the praedaren possess a shark-like humanoid figure, complete with dorsal fin, gills, and a large pair of beady, 'soulless' eyes. Their tails, which extend from their lower back, are powerful and used for propelling them quickly through the water, and their jaws are lined with rows of teeth that rapidly grow back when lost. Their placoid scaled skin is thick and textured like sandstone, but it is easily and often scarred. The praedaren's scars are worn with pride, and never hidden, for each tells a story of a battle that its bearer survived.

# Brief History
It was inevitable that the Union would one day make contact with the praedaren. The Union set its eyes upon the Shattered Isles long ago, but to claim them it had to master the sea. The praedaren were born masters of the sea. A complacent race might not have pursued the sea beyond their natural advantages, but the praedaren are a different breed. There is no finer ship on the seas than a praedaren ship, and their is no finer sailor than a praedaren. Unfortunately for the Union they tend to use their advantages to plunder any Union vessel they see. 

# Rarity in the Union
Most of the time if a praedaren sets foot in the union, it is just a short jaunt to burn a dock and steal some cargo. It is at most a few score that remain in the Union long term.

# Political Standing in the Union
The only political power the praedaren tend to hold in the Union is what they can leverage through the taking of hostages, and crippling of trade routes.

# Reputation in the Union
The praedaren are generally met with fear. They are known as terrors of the sea, and many praedaren are all to happy to reinforce that image.
