# Description
Erina are a small-statured race of creatures resembling bipedal hedgehogs. The average erina stands about 3 feet tall and weighs only 50 pounds. Erina have sharp spines that start at their scalp and run down their backs, spines also protrude from their forearms, the rest of their body is covered in soft fur that can range from snow white to deep brown.

# Brief History
The erina are a surprisingly hardy folk. Where other smaller races would had died to the varied predators of the Shattered Isles, the erina have survived, and even thrived. They can be found on almost any of the islands in the isles, though they prefer to keep to their own isolated settlements. Despite this many erina do venture out of their settlements, either to trade, find a new place to settle, or even find adventure.

# Rarity in the Union
The erina are very rare in the Union. Few erina have made the journey, and most walk the Union in search of a place to settle.

# Political Standing in the Union
The erina are largely ignored by politics, and likely will continue to be so long as they keep to themselves.

# Reputation in the Union
Erina are generally seen as enigmatic travellers in the Union, rarely does an erina spend any length of time around civilisation.
