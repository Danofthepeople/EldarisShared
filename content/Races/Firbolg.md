# Description
Firbolgs are tall, broad-shouldered humanoids, standing between 7 to 8 feet tall, with powerful yet gentle builds that exude a quiet strength. Their skin ranges from earthy tones of soft blues and greys to warm browns, often complemented by thick, shaggy hair or beards that give them an almost ancient, druidic appearance. Their large, expressive eyes hold a calm wisdom, and their broad noses and slightly elongated ears hint at their deep connection to the natural world. Though they are massive in stature, firbolgs move with remarkable grace and careful intent, each step seeming deliberate and measured, as if in tune with the rhythms of the earth. Their voices are deep and soothing, carrying the weight of ages, yet they often speak softly, preferring action and understanding over unnecessary words.

# Brief History
Not much is known of the reclusive firbolgs. Their origin is a mystery in itself, they were clearly once giant kin, and natives of the material plane. At some point during their history the entirety of the firbolg people were changed. Fundamentally altered by the magic of the feywild. The most popular theory is that a band of giant kin found themselves transported to the feywild, and over long years the plane twisted their form to what we know today. There is no official record of this however, if the firbolgs know their origin it is a closely guarded secret. 

The union first became aware of firbolgs in one of their many confrontations with the druid clans. The firbolgs were natural druids, yet did not seem to play an overly significant role in the clans. Interrogation revealed that the firbolg's way of life simply aligned with the clan's desire to protect nature. Hundreds of years later the firbolgs are still a reclusive people, living in isolated forest clans, rarely making their presence known to the union. The simple forest life is enough for most firbolgs, and the union is content to leave them be.

# Rarity in the Union
Firbolgs are very rare in the union. The only ones that are ever be seen are those looking to trade for the clans, and outcasts.

# Political Standing in the Union
Firbolgs ignore the politics of the union as much as it ignores them.

# Reputation in the Union
Firbolgs are a wondrous sight to most in the union, though their size can often make them intimidating despite their calm nature.
