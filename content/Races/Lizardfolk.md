# Description
Lizardfolk are tall, lean, and powerfully built reptilian humanoids, typically standing between 6 to 7 feet tall, with sleek, muscular frames built for both strength and agility. Their bodies are covered in scaly hides, ranging in color from deep green to muddy brown, gray, or even hints of blue, often patterned for natural camouflage. They have elongated snouts filled with sharp teeth, slit-pupiled eyes that gleam with cold, predatory focus, and ridged or crested heads that can vary between individuals. A long, thick tail extends from their spine, aiding in balance and swimming, while their clawed hands and feet make them adept at both combat and survival in the wild. Lizardfolk are stoic and difficult to read, their expressions subtle and their movements deliberate, efficient, and always with purpose, reflecting their pragmatic and instinct-driven nature.

# Brief History
Nobody knows quite where the lizardfolk came from, only that they started appearing some time after the first Dawn of Ascension. The emotionless beings seemed content to lead simple, mundane lives. Surviving off the land, and never thinking too far ahead. They likely would have stayed that way, but the dragons had other plans. 

Over long generations the lizardfolk were taught that, to survive, they must worship and serve the dragons. The dragons effectively turned the race into a self replicating, remorseless army to fight in their wars. For millennia the lizardfolk have fought in battles they don't believe in, for the pride of dragons that subjugate them. Thousands of their own are slaughtered each year and the fighting never slows. The lucky ones are kept as breeding stock. 

Now that dragon gods [[Udremm]] and [[Imrag]] are at war, it seems that war is all lizardfolk will ever know. Most lizardfolk in the union act as personal security for visiting dragonborn, or are sold as soldiers

# Rarity in the Union
Lizardfolk are rare in the union, with most being brought over by the dragonborn clans.

# Political Standing in the Union
Lizardfolk have no political power in the union, or interest in it. Their focus tends to be more on the direct future.

# Reputation in the Union
Lizardfolk are met with caution by most in the union, especially when they look hungry.
