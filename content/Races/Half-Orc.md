# Description
Half-orcs possess a strong, well-built physique, inheriting the imposing stature and endurance of their orcish ancestry while blending the versatility and varied features of their human lineage. Their skin tones range from deep gray-greens to more muted earthen hues, and their features are bold and well-defined, with strong jawlines and slightly pronounced lower tusks that hint at their heritage. Their eyes, often sharp and piercing, reflect a deep sense of focus and purpose, traits highly valued in orcish culture. While naturally muscular and resilient, half-orcs carry themselves with a measured confidence, their movements thoughtful and deliberate rather than brutish. Their presence commands respect, not through intimidation, but through an aura of discipline, strength, and quiet resolve, embodying the meritocratic ideals of their orcish ancestors.

# Brief History
When two races meet there are many possibilities, violence used to be the only option for humans and orcs. However in modern Eldaris on rare occasions human and orc societies will link. New couples form, and sometimes bear a half-orc child. A half-orc is likely to know both it's parents, as orcs rarely split from their chosen partner. Even in cases of a split, the orcish parent always ensures the child knows who their family is. This tends to give half-orcs strong familial bonds, and a powerful support structure to propel them into life. 

Half-Orcs still face many difficulties in life however. Their orcish heritage is a sticking point for many. They also are susceptible in some way to [[Tadon|Tadon's]] fury as their orcish parents are, but to a lesser extent. Due to this it can be hard for half-orcs to integrate into society. For those looking for something more in life, the climb is a steep one with many pitfalls, thankfully with the strengths of their parents, half-orcs always get back up. 

# Rarity in the Union
Half-Orcs exceedingly rare in the union, as birthrates are incredibly low. Further the race is not stable, as each generation the line tends back towards human or orc. No attempts have been made to stabilize the race.

# Political Standing in the Union
Half-Orcs, in terms of politics, get all of the drawbacks of being an orc, and few of the benefits of being human.

# Reputation in the Union
Half-Orcs are generally met with concern by citizens of the union, but less hostility than their orcish parents.

# Mechanics
