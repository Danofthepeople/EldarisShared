# Description
Fused are a striking blend of human forms enhanced by unnatural, bio-magical adaptations, their bodies altered through ancient rituals or deliberate experimentation. While their base features often resemble humanity, they bear distinctive grafts of animalistic or aquatic traits, such as chitinous plating, webbed hands, additional limbs, or bioluminescent markings. Their eyes may gleam with an unsettling, inhuman glow, and their movements carry an uncanny fluidity or precision, shaped by the nature of their enhancements. No two Fused look exactly alike, as their transformations are unique to their lineage or purpose.

# Brief History
It is not only gods who can manipulate the weave of humanity, in the last two hundred years mortals have discovered the practice themselves. In the 3rd era, the greatest minds of vedalken, humanity and elves were gathered by the Divine Order. They were given a project, with the goal of adapting the populous to the varied environments of Eldaris.

They started with humans, the race most compatible with alterations to their form. The project returned few viable specimens, the rest died during the process. It became clear that only specific candidates would ever produce results, meaning that the project would never be viable for the unions expansion efforts.

Quickly funding was reallocated, scientists were dispersed, and the project was swept away. Some however could not accept the loss of their life's work, and to this day work to prove the order wrong, but now with more extreme mutations. The only evidence of their work are the sporadic kidnappings of adult humans, and the occasional failed attempt that gets loose.

# Rarity in the Union
The fused are near mythical in the union, officially they do not exist. Even the most optimistic models only predict a handful of successful cases.

# Political Standing in the Union
Fused would not really factor into politics at all. They are little more than a footnote even to those with extensive knowledge.

# Reputation in the Union
Fused, if anything, would likely receive the same treatment as shifters. Due to the physical changes there is a high chance that even your own family and friends would not recognise you, and if they do it's unlikely they'll accept you.

# Mechanics
The rules for this race can be found in the Roll20 compendium, under the name Simic Hybrid.

Fused are thought to share the same weakness to silver that shifters have, due to their bestial nature. In reality they have no such weakness.

As a fused your character would have had a normal life, until they were kidnapped. After which they would remember being abandoned outside of civilisation, changed beyond recognition.
