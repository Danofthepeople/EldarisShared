# Description
Yuan-ti were originally humans who transformed themselves into serpent folk through an ancient bargain. Most yuan-ti were corrupted into monsters by that deal, but some yuan-ti instead became a new people who mix characteristics of humans and snakes.

Blessed with resistance to magical and poisonous effects by the bargain that created them, each of these yuan-ti manifests their serpentine heritage in a variety of ways: a forked tongue, snake eyes, a snakelike nose, or some other ophidian characteristic. However a yuan-ti looks, they have the power to pursue great good or evil in Eldaris.

# Brief History
When faced with fear mortals have few choices, stand with courage in the face of it, or cower before it. One group however made a different choice, for you can have no fear if you are fear itself. This group made a deal with Servillah, one that would free them from the petty concerns of fear and empathy. One that made them agents of fear, spreading Servillah's will. This group became the yuan-ti. Unlike many gods, Servillah knows the minds of mortals well. She knew that binding mortals, forcing them to enact her desires, would only be temporary. The changes she made to the yuan-ti meant that they felt no devotion to her. The arrangement needed to be an exchange, a simple transaction. Service now and always, for great power and wealth. The calculating minds of the yuan-ti agreed to the terms without hesitation, just as Servillah knew they would. Very few yuan-ti have ever broken this bond, and over the years their service has been rewarded. They may never have a place in the union, but a snake cares not for the feelings of it's prey.

# Rarity in the Union
Yuan-ti are incredibly rare in the union, very few choose to integrate into society. Most avoid the union entirely, staying with their own kind where they are the ruling class.

# Political Standing in the Union
Yuan-ti are politically opposed to the union, and have been outright enemies at times. Consequently yuan-ti have few rights, and little protection in the law.

# Reputation in the Union
Yuan-ti are often treated as monsters. Thus in the union they tend to stick to the darker underside, where a lack of empathy is sought after trait.
