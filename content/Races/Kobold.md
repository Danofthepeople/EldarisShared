# Description
Kobolds are small, wiry reptilian humanoids, standing between 2 to 3.5 feet tall, with slender but dexterous frames built for agility rather than strength. Their scaly hides come in shades of red, brown, green, or black, often matching the environments they inhabit. They have elongated snouts with sharp teeth, large, expressive eyes, and prominent head crests or frills that vary in shape. Their long, whip-like tails help with balance, and their clawed hands and feet make them adept at digging, climbing, and crafting intricate traps. Though physically small and unimposing, kobolds are quick, clever, and resourceful, moving with nervous energy and an ever-watchful awareness of their surroundings, always thinking two steps ahead for survival.

# Brief History
When [[Udremm]] ascended to godhood he scattered eggs across Teitsa'Var. Though as he reached the end of his ascension, [[Udremm|Udremm's]] burst of power waned. Causing the last of the eggs to shrink down, instead of bearing mighty dragonborn, these eggs bore the diminutive kobolds. 

Kobolds for much of their early existence were seen as failed dragonborn, and were expected to die off quickly. This did not happen however, and like rats soon there were kobolds everywhere in Teitsa'Var. Reluctantly kobolds were granted an official place in dragonborn society.

For the most part they were still treated like dirt, and could hold no real power, but they were at least protected. Eventually every home, rich or poor, had kobold servants. Any job considered below a dragonborn was given to the kobolds. Making them the expendable working class of Teitsa'Var. When the dragonborn headed for he Union, the kobolds followed. Most remain loyal to their clan and house, but some are brave enough to explore the new world.

# Rarity in the Union
Kobolds are rare in the union, with most sticking to the large cities.

# Political Standing in the Union
Kobolds have no political power in the union, generally relying on their dragonborn patrons to argue on their behalf.

# Reputation in the Union
Kobolds, to most in the union, are seen as just the meek servants of the dragonborn.
