# Description
Owlin are small to medium-sized, owl-like humanoids, their bodies cloaked in a soft layer of fine, downy feathers that range in colour from snowy white and ash grey to mottled brown and midnight black. Their round, expressive eyes dominate their faces, glowing faintly in the dark and capable of an unsettling, unblinking stare. Short, hooked beaks and expressive feathered brows give them a perpetually watchful and contemplative look. Their broad, silent wings fold neatly against their backs, allowing them to move through the air with graceful, ghost-like quiet.

# Brief History
Despite all appearances, the Owlin are in no way related to [[Aarakocra]]. In fact it generally believed that the Owlin have nothing to do with [[Udona]], instead being more closely aligned with [[Phisyn]]. The Owlin deny that any god played a role in their creation, and they have the historical documents to prove it. They are a studious race, whose home island is home to more libraries than taverns.

When the first Union ship made landfall on the Owlin home island, the crew was barely off the ship before the first Owlin reached them. The crew was prepared for violence, an uncivilised people, savages who would drive them from the shore. Instead they were met by bureaucrats who tied up the crew in lengthy discussion. By the time the discussion was complete the crew were entirely tied up in both Owlin and Union law. This initial encounter laid the framework for all future encounters with the Owlin, even the Unions bureaucratic engine falls apart under the wit of the Owlin.

# Rarity in the Union
Owlin are exceedingly rare in the union, especially outside of libraries and universities.

# Political Standing in the Union
The Owlin have fairly good political standing in the Union, largely because to them the Union's laws are simple and easily manipulated.

# Reputation in the Union
The Owlin have a well earned reputation for acting superior to everyone around them, this means they are generally disliked.
