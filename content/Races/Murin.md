# Description
Reaching a little over 2 feet tall, the mouse-like people often go unnoticed in their day-to-day activities. Their diminutive stature and quiet demeanour make the murin inoffensive to most, granting a certain detachment from the major conflicts and strife of the age. Their fast metabolisms and quick feet give the murin a slender build, usually weighing between 20-30 pounds.

Murin fur colour ranges from white to tan, to brown or black, with various patterns passed through heredity. They take care to groom and clean their fur, as it represents their family and community. A murin's eye colour is either red or black. Murin like to wear comfortable, dark-coloured clothes that allow for discretion and a wide range of movement.

# Brief History
The murin were only recently discovered by the Union, roughly thirty years ago. Which is interesting as they were discovered on an island that the Union had settled centuries ago. Following their discovery on the island, more and more isolated groups of murin have been discovered. The majority live on the islands but a few communities have been discovered on the Union mainland. Nobody knows how long these communities have existed, and just how many there are.

# Rarity in the Union
Thought to be very rare, but the murin are well known for being able to hide small communities in even the busiest of places.

# Political Standing in the Union
The murin in general have no interest in union politics, and it has no interest in them.

# Reputation in the Union
The murin are seen as a curious oddity. Their general cuteness means that in general they are looked upon fondly, if they are seen at all.
