# Description
Kenku are slender, raven-like humanoids, standing between 4 to 5 feet tall, with light, wiry frames built for agility and stealth. Their bodies are covered in sleek, black or dark brown feathers, giving them a shadowy, elusive presence. Though they lack wings, their arms end in dexterous, clawed hands, well-suited for delicate work, be it crafting, theft, or mimicry. Kenku often seem furtive and restless, their posture hunched and their heads constantly tilting as they absorb every sound and movement around them.

# Brief History
When [[Udona]] and [[Yrdis]] went to war, the aarakocra were forced to fight alongside their god. Some however detested being used as agents of war, and rebelled. In abandoning [[Udona]] they drew the gods wrath, and three curses were placed upon them. The first withered their wings, locking them out of [[Udona|Udona's]] domain. The second stole their creativity, and colour, rendering them dark copies of themselves. The final stole their voice, ensuring they would never speak out again. 

These cursed individuals were the start of the kenku race. They found their home on the surface of Eldaris, but the curses were slowing killing them. Every problem they faced was insurmountable, after all it's impossible to solve problems without original thoughts. In the end the kenku were saved by The Divine Order. They were given jobs, and homes. A place in society where their mimicry was valued, or easily exploited. To this day the curse holds strong, and the kenku are entirely dependent on those that would use them for their own gain. 

# Rarity in the Union
Kenku are uncommon in the union, most of the population is controlled by and works for The Divine Order.

# Political Standing in the Union
Kenku have no political power in the empire, their lack of creativity makes them awful politicians.

# Reputation in the Union
Kenku are seen as little more than a fact of life in the empire, nothing to remark upon, something to be ignored.

# Mechanics
The rules for this race can be found in the Roll20 compendium. 

All kenku would have been provided with an in-depth dialect by the Sect of Education. So you aren't limited to mimicking sounds as you will have a 'default' voice. This default voice is likely that of a teacher from the area you grew up.

Normally kenku have no ability for original thinking, however as a kenku adventurer you would be the one in a ten thousand exception to that rule.
