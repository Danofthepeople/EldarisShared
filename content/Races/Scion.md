# Description
Scions are physically indistinguishable from humans. Mentally they are vastly different. Each scion is bound to an ancestral spirit, which bears the knowledge generations past. This grants many scions great wisdom and intelligence. For others it grants madness as they plagued by the memories of long dead souls every time they sleep.

# Brief History
Scions are Phisyn's gift to humanity. Phisyn is the god of knowledge, and for millennia they have watched as information is lost as the wise die. Nothing pained Phisyn more than watching society fall backwards, having to reinvent the discoveries of their ancestors. Phisyn's solution was the ancestral spirit. Shortly after a human dies a ritual can be performed to capture a portion of their mind. If the human's soul is willing the process is non destructive, if they are not willing the memories can be extracted by force, risking damage to both the soul and knowledge it carries. If the process is successful the memories become their own entity, become an ancestral spirit. Alone the spirit is helpless, and will degrade overtime. Thus it is important to bind the spirit to a human child, the younger the human the greater the chance of success, the child need not be willing for this process. Once bound the child is forever changed, becoming a Scion, and gaining access to whispers of knowledge from their ancestral spirit.

# Rarity in the Union
Scions near mythical the Union as the process of creating them is very difficult. Further ancestral spirits are often fed the knowledge of many humans over their lifetime, meaning many humans are required to make a single scion. Additionally when a scion dies the spirit is unbound, and will not pass on to a new host without the appropriate ritual, thus many spirits have degraded away to nothing when a scion has died unexpectedly.

# Political Standing in the Union
Scions are treated as humans in terms of politics as, unless they reveal themselves, there is no sign of their true nature.

# Reputation in the Union
Scions can pass as humans in any area that doesn't know who they are. Where they are known as scions they are often seen as soothsayers, and are pestered by common folk and nobility alike for the wisdom of their ancestral spirit.
