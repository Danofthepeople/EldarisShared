# Description
Hobgoblins are tall, broad-shouldered goblinoids, standing between 5.5 to 6.5 feet tall, with muscular, disciplined builds suited for war and strategy. Their skin ranges from deep red to dusky orange, often marked with scars from battle or training. They have sharp, angular features, narrow eyes that gleam with intelligence and focus, and prominent, pointed ears that twitch at the slightest sound. Their posture is always upright and disciplined, reflecting their martial culture and sense of order. Hobgoblins move with measured precision, never wasting energy, and their expressions remain stern and calculating, making them appear imposing and unreadable to outsiders. Even in casual settings, they exude an air of authority and readiness, as if always prepared for the next battle or command.

# Brief History
Of all the goblinoid races hobgoblins were the closest to Ruin. They combine a brilliant level of marshal prowess, with an excellent grasp of battlefield command and strategy. The hobgoblins used this to their advantage while serving Ruin, dealing significant blows to the growing Union. Unfortunately for Ruin, as the most intelligent of his creations, the hobgoblins were the quickest to abandon him. Largely pulling out of Union to found their own civilisation. They knew that the key to longevity as a race was to give old wounds time to heal. For many years hobgoblins were not seen at all in Union, until late in the second era when the Union's own expansion rediscovered them. Instead of war emissaries were sent, deals were made and a truce established. Modern hobgoblins in the union fill many rolls, from foreign dignitaries, to merchants, to valued bodyguards. There is even a hobgoblin regiment in the Sect of Defence.  

# Rarity in the Union
Hobgoblin citizens are uncommon in the union, however any hobgoblin can in theory make a life in the union.

# Political Standing in the Union
Hobgoblins are quite active in the politics of the union, and have some sway as a sizeable foreign power.

# Reputation in the Union
Hobgoblins are only really seen in cities within the union, they have earned cautious respect from most citizens.
