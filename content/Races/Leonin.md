# Description
Leonin are towering, powerfully built feline humanoids, their bodies rippling with dense muscle and raw physicality, built for violence and endurance. Their thick manes and sharp, predatory eyes give them a regal yet menacing presence, while their razor-edged claws and bone-crushing jaws make them natural-born killers. Every movement is efficient, calculated, and brimming with suppressed aggression, their instinctual savagery barely restrained beneath the weight of captivity. Though caged, their fury never fades, and beneath their chains lingers the ever-present threat of rebellion, a beast waiting for the moment to strike.

# Brief History
The leonin had perhaps the darkest, and most bloody introduction to the union. An introduction that soured the union's approach to all outside races. It began with a single colony ship heading towards an island they had tentatively named Varrenstead. That ship never reconnected with the union. 

The union sent two more ships to Varrenstead, small vessels owned by the Sect of Defence. On arrival they found the colony slaughtered. Wounds indicated savage beasts had befallen the colony, slaying and feasting upon it's people. One ship was sent to carry the message to union, the other fortified to watch. Not three hours after the first ship left, the second was assaulted by the leonin. They attacked without warning, savagely killing all they found, and this time burning everything to the ground. The first ship saw the flames, knowing they'd be too late to help their friends, they sailed on. Carrying the truth of what happened to the council. 

The council mustered a small fleet, sending a thousand soldiers, and one of their exalted to put an end to the savages. Though the exalted was an unstoppable  foe, and the union soldiers were well trained, the leonin fought to the last. The only remnants of their people, the cubs they left behind. 

# Rarity in the Union
Leonin are exceedingly rare in the union. They are all prisoners of state, and are the descendants of cubs taken from Varrenstead. It is very rare that one is deemed civilised enough to be freed.

# Political Standing in the Union
Leonin are completely dependant on state, wielding no political power themselves.

# Reputation in the Union
Leonin are seen as savages by most, and the leonin tend to reinforce that image at every opportunity.
