# Description
Boondaburrans are a platypus-like shorter, stout folk adapted for life along the waterways. Webbed hands and feet, a water repellent fur covering their form, small eyes, and a soft skin covered bill make them perhaps one of the strangest looking folk you could meet. However they are talented swimmers, excellent spear fishers, and more than capable of moving unnoticed through their natural environment.

# Brief History
Boondaburrans raise many questions. Like, what even are they? Much like platypus, boondaburran biology defies all logic. It is theorised that as Eldaris weaves new races into beings, left over components are slowly gathered. The boondaburrans is what happens when Eldaris gathers enough of these components to assemble into a roughly coherent whole. 

This is the only reason scholars can come up with for why a warm blooded, duck-billed, furred and venomous humanoid, with the ability to sweat milk, lay eggs and sense electricity exists. Thankfully, boondaburrans are pretty chill. They are more than happy to help scholars through mental breakdowns incurred by studying their physiology.

# Rarity in the Union
Boondaburrans are rare in the Union, with most visiting to scare biologists.

# Political Standing in the Union
The boondaburrans have no political power in the Union, and the Union does not want to accept that they exist.

# Reputation in the Union
Most people view boondaburrans as kind and calm. It is only those with the vaguest notions of what should and shouldn't be biologically possible that have issues with them.
