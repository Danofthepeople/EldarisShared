# Description
Halflings are small, sturdy humanoids, standing between 3 to 3.5 feet tall, with compact, well-proportioned builds that favour agility over strength. Their faces are warm and expressive, with round cheeks, bright eyes, and wide smiles that reflect their naturally cheerful demeanour. Their skin ranges from fair to rich earthy tones, often complemented by thick, wavy hair that grows in dark shades, and their feet are covered in a light layer of fur, allowing them to move comfortably barefoot. Halflings possess a graceful, effortless way of moving, blending quiet stealth with casual ease, as if they were made to slip through the world unnoticed when they choose. Though physically unassuming, there is an unmistakable spark of resilience and cleverness in them, a reminder that beneath their friendly nature lies a people who have survived and thrived through adaptability, wit, and an unshakable love for life’s simple joys.

# Brief History
After the first Dawn of Ascension, humans and dwarves were trying to form The Divine Union. Since the last major engagement was all out war, the two races were having difficulty finding common ground. The legend goes that the problem was solved when a halfling walked into a meeting of the first divines, with a tray of freshly baked muffins, and a smile on his face. This halfling of yore played mediator to the divines, and invited them home for tea. The legend doesn't state what was served for tea, however it is thought that round this halflings dinner table The Divine Union began. 

To this day nobody knows quite where the halflings came from, but all are grateful for their presence. As the empire has grown halflings have been with it every step of the way. They are the jovial warm heart of a rapidly expanding society. Pulling varied people together with warm food, fanciful tales, and a kind smile. Some in the union believe that the union would collapse without the halflings, and perhaps they are right.

# Rarity in the Union
Halflings are common in the union, they are welcomed into almost any community, and do a great job of slotting into daily life.

# Political Standing in the Union
Halflings have quite a lot of political power in the empire, mostly from acting as proxies for other races.

# Reputation in the Union
Halflings are almost universally loved in the empire, they are quite often seen as the social glue that keeps everything together.
