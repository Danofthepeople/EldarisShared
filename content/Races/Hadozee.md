# Description
Hadozee are nimble, simian humanoids with long, dexterous limbs and a light, agile frame built for life among the sails and rigging of ships. Their bodies are covered in short fur, ranging from deep browns to sandy golds, while their expressive faces, framed by small, rounded ears, bear keen eyes that gleam with curiosity and cunning. Their most distinctive feature is the loose membrane of skin stretching from wrist to ankle, allowing them to glide effortlessly through the air, a natural gift that makes them excellent climbers and daring acrobats. Their long, prehensile fingers and toes grip with uncanny precision, granting them an ease of movement few other races can match. Whether swinging through the rigging or leaping from mast to mast, the hadozee move with an effortless confidence that speaks to generations spent riding the wind.

# Brief History
The hadozee arrived in Eldaris alongside the giff, not as conquerors, but as refugees. Their home world, a hostile, dying place of endless storms and barren coasts, had long struggled to sustain them, and it was only by the mercy of the giff fleet that they escaped its ruin. Taken in by their saviours, many hadozee remain loyal to the fleet, serving as agile crew members and scouts, their natural gliding ability making them invaluable aboard the great vessels. Yet not all hadozee remain bound to the giff. As master sailors and daring navigators, they have found homes on ships across the Shattered Isles, their skill and fearlessness making them prized crewmates regardless of whose banner flies above them. To the hadozee, the sea and sky are freedom, and no ship that values swift hands and sharp wits ever turns one away.

# Rarity in the Union
The hadozee are rare in the Union, but wherever there are ships you are bound to see one.

# Political Standing in the Union
Whilst they do not have and active political sway, the hadozee are favoured by Union politics.

# Reputation in the Union
The hadozee have a reputation for being dependable and skilled. They also have a reputation for trying to keep up with other sailors in port taverns, and going on clumsy flights thereafter.
