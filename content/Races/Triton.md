# Description
Tritons are tall, regal aquatic humanoids, standing between 5 to 6 feet tall, with sleek, muscular builds adapted for life beneath the waves. Their scaled skin, ranging in hues of deep blues, teals, and sea-greens, shimmers faintly in sunlight. Webbed hands and feet grant them effortless grace in water. Fin-like crests adorn their heads, arms, or legs, varying in shape and size between individuals. Their large, luminous eyes are well-suited for the depths, giving them an almost otherworldly intensity. 

# Brief History
[[Yrdis]] had the dream of an empire on the sea floor. Intelligent beings like those on the land, that would be the sole masters of their domain, the rulers of the sea. During the first Dawn of Ascension [[Yrdis|Yrdis's]] dream was made manifest, the accidental beginning of the triton race. 

The triton were given many advantages in their early development. The dream they were conceived from gave them a beautiful capital city. The seed from which their empire grew. With no competition the tritons thrived, until the second battle between [[Yrdis]] and Udona. All triton fought to protect their home, but once the war was done the race was forever changed. 

With no enemies left to fight, they fought each other. The perfect empire was sundered, their divine capital city lost to time, now only a fragmented people remains. Late in the second era one fragment of the old empire found ships sailing their water. The union had stumbled upon them, and the triton were not about to freely share their kingdom. In modern Eldaris the union and the triton have mostly agreed terms, but each year the triton push for more.

# Rarity in the Union
Triton are rare in the union, with most staying in their underwater empire, though in coastal settlements it is not too unusual to come across a triton.

# Political Standing in the Union
Triton do have some level of political power over the empire. Though this comes mainly from the empires naval interests, and the triton's threats against them.

# Reputation in the Union
Triton through some fault of their own are seen as pompous and rude by many in the empire.
