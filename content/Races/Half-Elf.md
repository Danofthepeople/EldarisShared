# Description
Half-elves are a blend of human and elven heritage, possessing features that make them stand out among both races. They typically have the slender, graceful build of their elven ancestors but with the sturdier frame and varied physiques of humans. Their ears are slightly pointed, though not as long or pronounced as a full elf’s, and their eyes often hold a faint glimmer of otherworldly depth. Half-elves can inherit a wide range of skin tones, hair colors, and facial features, reflecting the diversity of their mixed lineage. While some may have the ageless beauty of elves, others bear a more rugged, adaptable look akin to humans. This duality gives them an unmistakable yet versatile appearance, allowing them to blend between cultures, though they are often seen as outsiders to both.

# Brief History
When two races meet there are many possibilities, sex is one of the more popular options. Rarely when a human and an elf couple a half-elf can be born. This likely would come as a shock to either party. Depending on their mother's race the life of a half-elf can vary greatly. 

With a human mother there is a good chance the half-elf never got to know their father. They would be thrust into the human life, with a busy focus on forging a better life for those who come next. With an elven mother it is unlikely that their mum even remembers their dad. Their life would become nomadic, with a steady focus on excellence of self. 

In both cases half-elves often feel out of place, wanting to live a calmer life than a humans, but yearning for more action than elves do. Though their parents may try, they rarely understand. In society there is no place for half-elves, they are just another oddity. This causes most half-elves to strike out on their own, to claim their independence and identity.

# Rarity in the Union
Half-Elves are exceedingly rare in the union, as birthrates are incredibly low. Further the race is not stable, as each generation the line tends back towards human or elf. Some small enclaves of half-elves have tried to stabilise their race, with little success.

# Political Standing in the Union
Half-Elves are expected to follow the politics of their parent races, though they rarely receive the benefits. No special accommodations have ever been made for the race, as they are seen as a rare blip.

# Reputation in the Union
Half-Elves are generally seen as oddities by citizens of the union. They often receive pity from the other races, though most refuse to care beyond that.
