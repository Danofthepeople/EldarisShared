# Description
Infused with the magic of the feywild, most fairies look like small, 1.5 to 3 feet tall, elves with insectile wings. Each fairy has their own physical quirks to set them apart from their peers. These quirks can range from having exceptionally large ears, to having a spectral unicorn horn, or even having insectile legs. Despite these often drastic differences, fairies know a member of their own race on sight, and generally accept their differences.

# Brief History
Fairies were generally unseen in the material plane until the last year of the 3rd era of the Divine Council, though they were not uncommon in the feywild. One fairy in particular, Mountain, the fey lord of chaos, is thought to be the first fairy to visit the material plane. This is likely hyperbole, however his prominence and fame among his own kind led others to follow in his footsteps.

# Rarity in the Union
Fairies are exceedingly rare in the union. It is only the adventurous ones that follow in the footsteps, or wingbeats, of Mountain.

# Political Standing in the Union
Fairies are too few to have any political sway, but are looked upon favourably by the union.

# Reputation in the Union
Fairies that visit the material plane usually do so for adventure, thus to the people of the Union they are generally seen as potential heroes.
