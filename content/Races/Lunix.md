# Description
The lunix are a race of wolf-like humanoids. They stand between 6 and 7 feet tall, though their heads are often hunched forwards, making them appear shorter. Lunix are covered in coarse fur ranging from silvery grey to deep black, occasionally marked with streaks of white or russet that reflect their familial lineages. Their elongated muzzles house sharp teeth suited for both intimidation and combat, while their forward-facing eyes give off a predatory intelligence.

# Brief History
The Unions first introduction to the lunix were when Kagranak, the Black Bear, invaded the Union. The lunix formed the bulk of his forces. Whilst not organized like a traditional army, their hunting packs proved to be eerily effective at picking apart Union defences. Where the lunix came from originally is unknown, though their affinity with the moon hints at the goddess [[Aelia|Aelia's]] influence.

# Rarity in the Union
Lunix are rare in the union outside of Vygofeln, which was conquered by Kagranak.

# Political Standing in the Union
The lunix are regarded as enemy soldiers in a time of peace, whilst there is animosity care is taken on both sides not to antagonise.

# Reputation in the Union
Lunix are seen by many as the enemy, and thus tend to strike fear into those who see them, especially when they travel in packs.
