# Description
Harengon are lithe, long-limbed humanoids with the powerful legs and keen senses of a hare, built for speed and agility. Their fur, which ranges from snowy white to rich browns and deep blacks, provides natural camouflage in various environments, while their tall, expressive ears swivel to catch even the faintest sounds. Their sharp eyes and twitching noses give them an ever-alert appearance, always scanning for danger or opportunity. Though lean, their bodies are packed with coiled muscle, allowing them to leap great distances with ease. Harengon hands are nimble and precise, well-suited for quick work, whether in combat, craftsmanship, or games of dexterity.

# Brief History
When the Union’s colonists first arrived in the Shattered Isles, they found the harengon confined to a single large island, their society deeply rooted in its rolling hills and dense forests. Lacking naval technology, they had never ventured beyond their homeland’s shores, living in tightly knit warrens and villages that thrived through speed, cunning, and communal cooperation. Though less advanced than the Union, their knowledge of the land made them invaluable guides, scouts, and messengers. The arrival of the Union changed everything, some harengon willingly embraced trade and opportunity, while others were carried to new shores as laborers or stowaways. In time, their natural adaptability ensured they could be found across the Isles, but many still long for the days when their people knew only one home.

# Rarity in the Union
Harengon are rare in the Union. Few have been brought to the mainland, and most who have been long to return to the Isles.

# Political Standing in the Union
The Union considers the harengon to be a ward of the Union, a race that the Union uplifted and takes resposibility for.

# Reputation in the Union
The harengon have a good reputation in the Union, getting on with most races. They share an especially strong bond with halflings, who similarlly are liked by all.
