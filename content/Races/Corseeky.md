# Description
Corseeky are tall, imposing constructs of metal, stone, and wood, standing between 6 to 7 feet tall, with broad, powerful frames designed for resilience and function. Their bodies are a seamless fusion of artifice and purpose, with intricately crafted plating serving as both armour and skin, often etched with faint sigils or markings the sovereign. Their faces are smooth and expressionless, save for the glowing eyes that burn with awareness, their colour shifting subtly with emotion and thought. Though their forms are rigid and unyielding, corseeky move with surprising fluidity, each motion deliberate and efficient. Their voices resonate with a metallic timbre, calm and steady, carrying the weight of their existence. Despite their inorganic nature, time leaves its mark on them; scratches, dents, and weathering tell their stories, making each corseeky as unique as any mortal being.

# Brief History
The corseeky may be the oldest of the prime races. In fact it is thought that they may have existed during the forging of Eldaris, though no proof of this has ever been found or provided. The corseeky hive refuses to confirm or deny this, indeed the hive refuses to share almost anything about their race. Nobody knows where the bulk of the corseeky population resides. There are Corseeky towers that are thought to stretch deep underground, all who try to investigate further are taken inside, and never return. 

Only two types of corseeky live among other races:

The first are agents of the hive, sent into the world to serve a specific person or group. How it is decided who the corseeky will serve is unknown, but they serve totally until dismissed. Corseeky servants cannot be bribed, and have no will outside of what their patron demands of them. A corseeky servant was once seen as a symbol of great status, since the attempted cleansing of Eldaris by the hive, a corseeky servant are generally regarded as bad omens. 

The second type of corseeky servants is the snapped. An individual corseeky may snap in times of great emotional or physical distress. The process of snapping has no outward signs, the only way a non corseeky could tell is if the snapped individual reacted poorly. Once snapped the corseeky immediately gains free will, but loses all memory of the hive and their connection to it. Some enact revenge on their patrons, others continue as if nothing has happened, many simply leave to start their new life. Many corseeky snapped during the cleansing.

# Rarity in the Union
Corseeky servants are exceedingly rare in the union, they are often rejected by their patrons, and forced to return to the hive. Snapped corseeky are more common, with the horrors of the cleansing having forced free will upon many of them.

# Political Standing in the Union
Corseeky aligned with the hive are political enemies of the union. Snapped corseeky are begrudgingly accepted of refugees with no politcal sway.

# Reputation in the Union
Corseeky are met with open fear across Eldaris, the cleansing is still fresh in the minds of many.

# Mechanics
The rules for this race can be found in the Roll20 compendium, under the name Warforged. 

To play as a corseeky you must have snapped, and lost your connection to the hive. It is important to consider how your character snapped, what emotional event forced free will upon them, the most likely candidate would be actions taken during the cleansing.
