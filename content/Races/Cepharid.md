# Description
Cepharid biology is alien to most humanoids, and mirrors that of cephalopods. They have eight tentacles, each capable of fine movements. Four of which protrude from their lower body, acting both as legs and feeding limbs. The other four extend radially from their upper body, these generally act as arms, and branch into four smaller tentacles at the ends for fine manipulation. Perhaps the most disturbing part of cepharid biology is their boneless nature, they can squeeze through any gap their beak can fit through, the sight of which is nauseating to most.

# Brief History
According to cepharid scholars, their species was not created by a god, nor did it arise naturally on Eldaris. The cepharid believe that they are the product of the dreams of a long dead entity, that lies somewhere deep beneath the Shattered Isles. True or not, cepharid origins have been lost to time.

In modern Eldaris, the cepharid are a disorganised people. They value curiosity, and one personal journey above all else. They have no cities, or cultural hubs. Cepharid only come together to breed, afterwards they each return to their solitary journeys. In most races this would spell doom, as both population and culture withered away. However, for the cepharid this does not appear to be the case.

# Rarity in the Union
Cepharid are very rare in the Union, those who find their way there tend to do so to sate their boundless curiosity.

# Political Standing in the Union
The cepharid have no political power in the Union. They are generally seen as too disorganised to be of any concern.

# Reputation in the Union
The cepharid tend to cause instinctual revulsion in those they meet that can be hard to overcome.
