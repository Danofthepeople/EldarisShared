# Description
Gith are tall, gaunt humanoids, standing between 5.5 to 6.5 feet tall, with lithe, sinewy frames built for speed and precision. Their yellow-green skin is stretched taut over sharp, angular features, with high cheekbones, narrow eyes, and elongated, pointed ears that give them an alien, almost regal appearance. Though they move with graceful efficiency, there is always a coiled tension beneath the surface, as if they are constantly prepared to strike.

# Brief History
Two centuries ago the corseeky emerged en masse, escorting a new exotic race. The corseeky marched the gith to the major cities of The Divine Union, and implored it's leaders to accept the gith, or face war. At the time, no force had ever defeated the corseeky hive, so the union bent to the request. The action cost the union very little, due to the small population of gith being spread throughout the empire. The gith themselves seemed confused for the most part, but were happy to have a home. They have no memory of where they came from, but they were told by the corseeky they had fled to Eldaris. Most gith seem to have some form of agoraphobia, and inexplicably this phobia seems to pass to their children. In particular ships, and high places, cause gith heightened levels of distress. A further mystery is their innate psionic abilities. Another is their two clear social groups, one being masters of all things martial, the other being incredibly keen of mind. With all the questions surrounding the gith they have become popular amongst curios scholars.

# Rarity in the Union
Gith are rare in the union. They can find livelihoods almost anywhere, however they tend to isolate themselves to gith communities.

# Political Standing in the Union
Gith are a political mystery, nobody knows quite what they want, including the gith. For the most part they are left to their own devices.

# Reputation in the Union
Gith are seen as mysterious inscrutable creatures by most in the union, but are accepted as one of lifes many oddities.
