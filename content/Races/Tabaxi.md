# Description
Tabaxi are graceful, feline humanoids, their lean, agile frames built for speed and precision. Covered in sleek fur that ranges from spotted and striped patterns to solid tones of tawny, black, or white, they move with an effortless, predatory grace, every step near-silent. Their keen, slitted eyes shimmer with curiosity, constantly scanning their surroundings, while their whiskered faces and twitching ears make them appear ever-alert. A long, expressive tail flicks behind them, helping them maintain balance whether climbing, sprinting, or stalking unseen in the shadows. Though their movements are light and quick, their clawed hands and feet reveal their potential for sudden, explosive bursts of action—whether in combat, a chase, or a desperate bid for treasure.

# Brief History
The tabaxi hail from a tropical island known as Lady Felicity in the union. Though the tabaxi prefer to call it The Ever Hearth of Felicity. First contact with the tabaxi was difficult. The union had recently fought a war against the leonin, and the tabaxi looked much alike the savage leonin. At first it was assumed they would respond to the union with violence, forcing the union to establish a military presence on Felicity. In actuality the tabaxi simply ignored the presence of the union, spending their lives living off the land as they always had, until they found a better way. 

The union was wasteful, and somewhat careless despite the large number of soldiers. Some tabaxi saw this as an opportunity to purloin items from their new neighbours, gaining luxury with minimal effort. Overtime this practice became common place, some stealing to have an easy life, others to satisfy idle curiosity. When the union discovered this, they feared the worst and so reacted with force. Yet the tabaxi refused to fight, claiming that they had already saved the world once, and they were due a few more generations of easy living. 

It seemed to all involved that the tabaxi truly believed the world owed them a great debt. Though what they had actually done has been lost to time. While the bureaucratic cogs of the union turned, trying to decide the fate of this new people, the tabaxi stole themselves a new life. Slipping away in merchant ships, stealing whole vessels in some cases, to explore world they saved and live an easy life

# Rarity in the Union
Tabaxi are rare in the union, they are usually found travelling the union in small family groups, rarely settling down.

# Political Standing in the Union
Tabaxi have no political power in the union, and are well known for ignoring it's culture.

# Reputation in the Union
Tabaxi are met with distrust, and disdain. Their disregard for culture, and lax understanding of ownership is well known in the union.
