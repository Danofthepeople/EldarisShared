# Description
The myrmex are an insectoid race of eusocial creatures. The majority of the myrmex resemble ants, though they can range in size from as small as a grain of rice, to as large as a tavern. These myrmex exist to serve the colony unquestioningly. They have no true intelligence, simply following the pheromonal commands of their betters. 

When the colony requires myrmex with more than animalistic instinct, the queen can issue special pheromones to unhatched myrmex to create exilarchs. Exilarchs are granted sapience and independence, akin to that of an average humanoid. There are many forms of exilarchs, depending on the colonies needs, but all possess a centaur-like build, with a horizontal, chitin-covered abdomen supported by four powerful legs and an upright humanoid torso with two clawed arms. Exilarchs act as explorers, diplomats, and warriors for their colonies, or as rogue agents seeking their own destinies.

# Brief History
It is unclear how long the myrmex have existed. It is believed that they lived underground for a substantial amount of time before surfacing across the Scattered Isles. Unfortunately the myrmex are not forthcoming with information, and any attempt to get a closer look at their colonies results in violence. The little that is known about them comes from trade, rogue exilarchs, and interrogations of captured agents.

Thankfully the myrmex seem largely content to stick to colony life. Given their incredibly adaptive biology, and impressive organizational capabilities, it is thought if they ever truly turn their eyes out to Eldaris, they would be unstoppable.

# Rarity in the Union
Myrmex are exceedingly rare in the Union, only a small handful of exilarchs may be found in the entire state.

# Political Standing in the Union
Myrmex are a subject of Union politics, but hold no power. They are considered to be a potential threat, should they ever turn their focus to war.

# Reputation in the Union
Myrmex have a strange reputation in the Union. All who meet an exilarch will speak highly of them, having unwittingly being influenced by their pheromones. Those who are aware of myrmex influence over others fear losing autonomy to them, and being forced to join a colony.
