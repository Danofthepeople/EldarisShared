# Description
Dwarves are stocky, broad-shouldered humanoids, standing between 4 to 5 feet tall, with dense, muscular builds that give them surprising strength and endurance. Their thick, hardy bones and compact frames make them naturally resilient, able to withstand harsh environments and gruelling labour. Dwarves have strong, angular features, with deep-set eyes, prominent brows, and stout noses, often framed by thick, meticulously groomed beards that are a point of pride for both men and women. Their skin ranges from pale to deep earth tones, reflecting the rugged mountains and underground halls they call home. Dwarves move with purpose and determination, each step firm and unwavering, embodying their stubborn resilience and tireless work ethic. Even in stillness, they exude an air of quiet strength, like stone waiting to endure the test of time.

# Brief History
Dwarvish histories claim that the race was born from the hewn stone of a great mountain. Each with a diamond for a heart, a mane of fire and a mind of steel. Whether the story is true has been lost to the ages, but the qualities do seem to match. Strong as stone, their will hard as diamond and minds keen as steel. Their mane of fire gives them the best beards of any race alive. Before the first contact war the dwarves developed quickly, their innate understanding of enchantment forging and construction propelling the race to new heights. While other races were making flint tipped spears, the dwarves were experimenting with metallurgy. When the first contact war broke out the dwarves had the advantage in technology.  After the war dwarves began to expand into new territories, slower than humans no doubt, but with better foundations. Even with selling their technology to the other prime races, the dwarvish empire was the most advanced by a significant margin. 

They would likely have become the dominant race if it were not for the first long night. As the most advanced and successful race the dwarves faced the brunt of the fiendish forces. Many dwarvish cities were lost entirely, while others were crippled to the point of unsustainability. At the end of the long night, during the first dawn of ascension, three dwarves ascended and became divines. Without these divines to unite the shattered remnants of dwarven kind, the race would have surely faded into history.   The dwarves, after near extinction, were all too happy to accept the human divine's offer of a union. So humanity and dwarven kind became inexorably linked. With dwarves building the great structures of the union, and arming it's people, clutching to life that now seemed so fragile.

# Reputation in the Union
Dwarves are respected by most races in the union, many defer to dwarves when looking for a level-headed opinion.

# Mechanics
The rules for this race can be found in the Roll20 compendium. 

Late in the 3rd era, dwarves suffered an infertility plague. Dwarves can no longer have children with each other in the traditional manner. Instead they follow a process shared with them by Ginmik Bronzearm. The exact process is a dwarven secret, but most know that a pair of group of dwarves comes together to carve a from stone, infusing it with their essence, and that stone becomes a flesh and blood new-born dwarf.
