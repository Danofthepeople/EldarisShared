# Description
Bomok are sturdy, canine-humanoids with keen senses and a natural air of vigilance. Their highly tuned ears and sharp noses make them exceptional trackers and sentinels, rarely caught off guard even in casual settings. Their fur can vary in length and colour, often reflecting their regional heritage, and they carry themselves with an alert, purposeful stance. While their builds tend to be robust and well-muscled, their agility and precision are just as notable as their strength.

# Brief History
The Union’s first encounters with the Bomok were not through whispers or fleeting shadows but in the form of steadfast sentries and disciplined warriors. As the Union expanded into the Shattered Isles, they found the bomok entrenched in the region’s cities, villages, and strongholds, serving as protectors, soldiers, and enforcers of local law. Unlike the more elusive inhabitants of the Isles, the bomok did not shy away from the Union’s presence, but neither did they embrace it. They watched the newcomers with measured wariness, allowing trade and diplomacy, but always with a hand on their weapons and a watchful eye.

It quickly became evident that the bomok honour-bound nature made them unlikely to strike without provocation, but when angered, they became a storm of fury and precision. A handful of early conflicts erupted when Union officers overstepped boundaries or treated their people with disrespect, resulting in brutal skirmishes where even outnumbered bomok warriors tore through ranks of soldiers with relentless efficiency. These incidents served as stark warnings, the bomok would tolerate outsiders, but only so long as they conducted themselves with respect and integrity. Over time, the Union came to regard them as both formidable obstacles and invaluable allies, recognizing that while bomok loyalty could not be bought, it could be earned.

# Rarity in the Union
Bomok are uncommon in the Union, but in major port cities, military outposts, and merchant hubs they are more common. Generally acting as guards, mercenaries, or tradesmen.

# Political Standing in the Union
Their reputation as loyal warriors, disciplined sentinels, and skilled artisans has earned them a place in Union society, particularly in military and security roles, but they have no centralized leadership or major political representation within the Union’s governing bodies.

# Reputation in the Union
The bomok are generally respected, admired, and sometimes feared within the Union. Their unwavering sense of loyalty and strong martial traditions make them ideal as guards, soldiers, and bodyguards, but their fierce intensity and rigid honour code can make them intimidating to the average citizen.
