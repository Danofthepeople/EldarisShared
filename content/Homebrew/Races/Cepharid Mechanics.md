# **Traits**
**Ability Score Increase.** You increase one ability score by 2 and a different one by 1.  
**Age.** Cepharids reach maturity at 15 years and can live up to 100 years.  
**Size.** Cepharids range from 4 to 6 feet tall, but their flexible bodies allow them to shift in size slightly. Your size is Medium.  
**Speed.** Your base walking speed is 25 feet, and you have a swimming speed of 35 feet.  
**Darkvision.** You have darkvision out to 60 feet, as your ancestors adapted to deep-sea darkness.  
**Amphibious.** You can breathe both air and water.
**Malleable Form.** Your body is boneless and malleable, you have advantage on checks to escape the Grappled and Restrained conditions. In addition, if you are not wearing armour, you can squeeze through gaps as small as 6 inches in diameter.
**Chromatic Camouflage.** You can subtly shift the pigments of your skin, allowing you to blend into your surroundings. You gain proficiency in the stealth skill, and can attempt to hide even when you have no cover, provided you are not in bright light.
**Eight Limbs.** You have eight tentacles, the four on your lower body act as legs, the four on your upper body act as arms. These extra limbs grant you the following benefits:
- You can interact with the world as if you had two extra fully functional arms. These arms do not grant any extra attacks, nor do they allow you to benefit from more than one shield at a time.
- You have advantage on checks made to grapple or restrain a creature.
- You have advantage on checks and saving throws against falling prone, or being forcibly moved.
- You have advantage on any checks or saving throws that involves clinging to a surface.
**Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Cepharid.
