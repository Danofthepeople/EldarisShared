# Traits
- **Ability Score Increase.** You increase one ability score by 2 and a different one by 1.  
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Darkvision.** Accustomed to life in underground colonies, you have darkvision out to 60 feet.
- **Insectoid Build.** You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
- **Pheromone Communication.** You can silently communicate simple ideas, emotions and sensations to any myrmex or creature with an Intelligence of 3 or higher within 60 feet. This ability does not work through magical barriers or airtight enclosures. This ability does not grant a creature the ability to respond in kind.
- **Hive Synergy.** When you are flanking a creature with an ally, both you and your ally gain an additional +1 bonus to attack rolls against the flanked creature. This bonus cannot stack.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Myrmex.

# Subraces
Myrmex colonies produce specialized castes designed for specific roles. As an Exilarch, you are blessed with sapience, and belong to one of these distinct groups.

## Bellator
Trained for war, these myrmex are heavily armoured and built for frontline combat.

- **Age.** Myrmex Bellator mature rapidly, reaching adulthood after 5 years, and can live up to 50 years.
- **Size.** Myrmex Bellator stand between 7 and 8 feet tall and are typically 7 to 9 feet long from head to abdomen. Your size is Medium.
- **Natural Armor.** Your exoskeleton is incredibly dense. Your natural AC equals 13 + your Constitution modifier. If you are wearing armour,  and your natural armour AC exceeds the AC provided by the armour, you may use your natural armour AC instead. You can benefit from a shield in addition to your natural armour.
- **Mandibular Strike.** Your powerful mandibles are natural weapons. You can use them to make unarmed strikes, dealing 1d8 + your Strength modifier piercing damage on a hit.
- **Resilient Physiology.** You have advantage on saving throws against poison, and resistance to poison damage.

## Operarius
Designed for labour, these myrmex excel in utility and support roles.

- **Age.** Myrmex Operarius mature rapidly, reaching adulthood after 5 years, and can live up to 50 years.
- **Size.** Myrmex Operarius stand between 4 and 5 feet tall and are typically 4 to 5 feet long from head to abdomen. Your size is medium.
- **Burrower.** You can burrow through soft earth and sand at a speed of 10 feet per round. You cannot burrow through solid rock.
- **Colony Coordination.** When you Help an ally with an ability check or attack roll, they gain an additional 1d4 to the roll. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.
- **Tireless.** You require only 4 hours of rest during a long rest, maintaining partial awareness during this period.

## Volans
These rare Myrmex are born with functional wings, allowing them to take to the sky.

- **Age.** Myrmex Volans mature exceedingly quickly, reaching adulthood after 1 year, and can live up to 25 years.
- **Size.** Myrmex Volans stand between 2 and 3 feet tall and are typically 2 to 3 feet long from head to abdomen. Your size is small.
- **Flight.** Because of your wings, you have a flying speed equal to your walking speed. You can’t use this flying speed if you’re wearing medium or heavy Armor.
- **Ariel Escape.** When a hostile creature creature ends its turn within 5 feet of you, you may use your reaction to fly up to half your speed in any direction, this movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

## Carnifex
These Myrmex are the executioners of the hive, designed to track down and eliminate defective, rogue, or treacherous members of the colony.

- **Age.** Myrmex Carnifex receive focused pheromones from the queen before they hatch, as a result they are born as adults, with a mind shaped by the queen. Carnifex live as long as the queen intends them to, though they must return to her at least once every 5 years or their bodies will fail.
- **Size.** Myrmex Carnifex stand between 6 and 7 feet tall and are typically 6 to 8 feet long from head to abdomen. Your size is medium.
- **Predatory Focus.** As a bonus action, you may focus your mind on one creature within 60 feet, attuning to their scent, movements and weak points. You gain the following benefits. Whilst the creature is within 1000 feet of you, you know its general direction. You add 1d4 to attack and damage rolls made against the creature. This effect lasts for one hour, or until you lose concentration, as if concentrating on a spell. This ability can be used a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.
- **Executioner’s Strikes.** When you score a critical hit with an unarmed strike or melee weapon, you can roll one of the unarmed strike's or weapon's damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage of the critical hit.
- **Silent Hunter.** You have proficiency in Stealth, and you can move at full speed while hidden without disadvantage on Stealth checks.

## Regina Exul
A queen without a colony, either hatched alone, abandoned, or forced into exile. Unlike true queens, you lack the ability to produce a full hive but retain the remnants of your royal authority and pheromonal command.

- **Age.** Myrmex Regina Exult very slowly, reaching adulthood after 30 years, and can live up to 1000 years.
- **Size.** Myrmex Regina Exul stand between 7 and 8 feet tall and are typically 7 to 9 feet long from head to abdomen. Your size is medium.
- **Command Pheromones.** As a bonus action, you can emulate the command spell, though this ability is non-magical. You may choose from Wisdom, Intelligence or Charisma to use as your spellcasting ability for the emulated command. This ability does not work through magical barriers or airtight enclosures. This ability can be used a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.
- **Royal Presence.** You gain proficiency in Persuasion or Intimidation (your choice).
- **Adaptive Pheromones.** If you spend 10 consecutive minutes within 60 feet of a creature, you can adapt your pheromones to better influence them, granting advantage on all Charisma checks against that creature as long as you are within 60 feet of them. This ability does not work through magical barriers or airtight enclosures. You may adapt your pheromones to influence a number of creatures equal to your proficiency modifier, if you adapt your pheromones to an additional creature, you must choose an exiting creature to lose pheromonal influence over.
