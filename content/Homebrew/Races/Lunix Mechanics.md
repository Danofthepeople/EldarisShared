# **Traits**
- **Ability Score Increase.** You increase one ability score by 2 and a different one by 1. 
- **Age.** Lunix mature quickly, reaching adulthood around 15 years old, and live up to 80 years.
- **Size.** Lunix stand between 5 and 7 feet tall and have lean, powerful builds. Your size is Medium.  
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.  
- **Darkvision.** Accustomed to hunting under the moon, you have darkvision out to 60 feet.  
- **Keen Senses.** You have proficiency in the Perception skill.  
- **Pack Tactics.** When an ally is within **5 feet** of a creature you are attacking, you gain advantage on attack rolls against that creature. 
- **Bite.** You have a fanged maw that you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with it, the strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier piercing damage, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Lunix.

# **Subraces**
Lunix can walk many paths in life, adapting to leadership, solitude, or spiritual callings.

## **Pack Leader**
Commanders of the hunt, Pack Leaders inspire their allies and keep the group unified.

- **Born Leader.** You gain proficiency in the Persuasion or Intimidation skill (your choice).  
- **Alpha’s Call.** As a bonus action, you can inspire an ally within 30 feet who can see or hear you. They gain temporary hit points equal to your level + your proficiency bonus. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.  


## **Lone Wolf**
Not all Lunix fight in packs, some roam alone, relying on sheer skill and tenacity.

- **Survival Instincts.** You gain proficiency in the Stealth or Survival skill (your choice).  
- **Feral Strikes.** Your bite now deals 1d8 piercing damage. In addition, When you hit with your bite, you can regain hit points equal to half the damage dealt. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.  

## **Moon Blessed**
Gifted with a spiritual connection to the moon, the Moon Blessed wield mystical power in battle.

- **Lunar Acuity.** You gain proficiency in the Insight or Arcana skill (your choice).  
- **Lunar Magic.** You learn the Light cantrip. At 3rd level, you can cast Faerie Fire once per long rest, you can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have of 1st level or higher. At 5th level, you can cast Moonbeam once per long rest, you can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have of 2nd level or higher. You may choose from Wisdom, Intelligence or Charisma to use as your spellcasting ability for these spells.
