A **Potion of Healing** restores HP based on **Potency** and gains additional effects based on **Harmony**. The **cost and risks** scale with quality.

# Potency Table
|**Potency**|**Healing Effect**|
|---|---|
|**P1**|1d2 HP|
|**P2**|1d4 + 1 HP|
|**P3**|2d4 + 2 HP|
|**P4**|4d4 + 4 HP|
|**P5**|8d4 + 8 HP|
|**P6**|10d4 + 20 HP|
|**P7**|15d4 + 40 HP|
# Harmony Benefits
|**Harmony**|**Effect**|
|---|---|
|**H6**|Grants **temporary HP** equal to **¼** of the HP restored (rounded up).|
|**H7**|Grants **temporary HP** equal to **½** of the HP restored (rounded up).|

# Withdrawal Effects
When suffering withdrawal from healing potions, your maximum HP is reduced depending on your addiction level. This cannot reduce your maximum HP below 1.

|**Addiction Level**|**Max HP Reduction**|
|---|---|
|**1**|1 HP|
|**2**|3 HP|
|**3**|7 HP|
|**4**|14 HP|
|**5**|28 HP|
|**6**|43 HP|
|**7**|71 HP
