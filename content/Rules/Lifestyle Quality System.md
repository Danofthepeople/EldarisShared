In your adventures, how well you rest and what you eat can affect your performance. When you take a Long Rest, the quality of your accommodations and food, determined by your Lifestyle, can grant minor bonuses or penalties until your next Long Rest.

A modest lifestyle is the baseline. It provides a standard night's rest with no bonuses or penalties.

Effects do not stack and reset after each long rest. Your current lifestyle tier determines the benefit or drawback you experience.

# Lifestyle Effects

| Lifestyle        | Effects (until next long rest)                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Wretched**     | You suffer -1 to all ability checks and saving throws.<br><br>Any healing you gain from hit dice during a short rest is halved.<br><br>You regain only half your maximum HP from a long rest.                                     |
| **Squalid**      | Any healing you gain from hit dice during a short rest is halved.<br><br>You regain only half your maximum HP from a long rest.                                                                                                   |
| **Poor**         | You regain only half your maximum HP from a long rest.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Modest**       | No effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Comfortable**  | After completing the long rest you gain temporary hit points equal to your character level.                                                                                                                                       |
| **Wealthy**      | Any healing you gain from hit dice during a short rest is doubled.<br><br>After completing the long rest you gain temporary hit points equal to your character level.                                                             |
| **Aristocratic** | You gain+1 to all ability checks and saving throws. <br><br>Any healing you gain from hit dice during a short rest is doubled.<br><br>After completing the long rest you gain temporary hit points equal to your character level. |
# Lifestyle Examples
|Lifestyle|Accommodations|Food & Drink|
|---|---|---|
|**Wretched**|Sleeping outdoors without shelter, gutters|Moldy bread, stagnant water, spoiled leftovers|
|**Squalid**|Infested flophouses, prison cells|Gruel, hardtack, cheap swill|
|**Poor**|Small room in a noisy inn, shared bed|Stew with questionable meat, watered ale|
|**Modest**|Private room in an inn, clean and basic|Hearty stew, bread, cheese, decent ale|
|**Comfortable**|Well-kept inn, soft bed, warm fire|Fresh meat, baked goods, wine or good ale|
|**Wealthy**|Fine suite, quality linens, bath service|Multiple-course meals, imported wine, exotic fruits|
|**Aristocratic**|Manor stay, servants, luxurious amenities|Gourmet meals, rare delicacies, aged spirits|
