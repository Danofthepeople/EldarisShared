# Backstory
I like a good backstory, and I will read any that are provided to me. However I appreciate backstories are hard to come up with. For this campaign I have come up with a framework to help you along.

## Character Questions
Please answer all of these questions.

### Personal History
- Where was your character born? What was their home like?
- Who raised them? Were they close to their family or estranged?
- Did they have any significant childhood experiences that shaped them?
- Did they grow up wealthy, poor, or somewhere in between?
- What kind of education or training did they receive?

### Motivations & Goals
- What is your character’s main goal in life? What drives them?
- What is something they fear or avoid at all costs?
- Do they have a personal code or set of morals they follow?
- What is something they want to accomplish before they die?

### Relationships & Connections
- Do they have any close friends, mentors, or rivals?
- Have they ever been in love or had their heart broken?
- Do they have any enemies or people they’ve wronged?
- How do they feel about the other members of the party?

### Skills & Abilities
- Where did they learn their primary skills (combat, magic, persuasion, etc.)?
- Do they have any unusual talents or hobbies?
- Have they ever had to rely on their wits to survive?

### Personality & Behaviour
- How do they react under pressure?
- What is their biggest strength and biggest flaw?
- Are they a leader, a follower, or a lone wolf?
- What is one thing that can make them lose their temper?

### Secrets & Conflicts
- Do they have a secret they don’t want anyone to know?
- Is there someone or something they are running from?
- Have they ever done something they deeply regret?
- What is a mistake from their past that still haunts them?

### The Call to Adventure
- Why did they leave their previous life to become an adventurer?
- What do they hope to find or achieve through adventuring?
- What would it take for them to settle down and stop adventuring?

## Chains of Connection
Each player character starts off knowing two other player characters in some way. This forms a chain of connection. The details of this relationship you will have to discuss with your paired players. At the very least you will answer the questions below, and share them with me.

```mermaid
 graph LR;
    P1 <-- Knows --> P2;
    P2 <-- Knows --> P3;
    P3 <-- Knows --> P4;
    P4 <-- Knows --> P5;
    P5 <-- Knows --> P1;
```

### How do you know each other?
- What past experiences or relationships tie you together?
- Did you meet during a shared event, through mutual friends, or another circumstance?

### How close are you?
- Are you old friends, reluctant allies, or rivals?
- Do you trust each other fully, or is there some tension?

### How do you treat the other person?
- Are you friendly, respectful, dismissive, or antagonistic?
- Do you have inside jokes or rivalries?

### What do you think of them?
- What are your opinions, fears, or hopes regarding these individuals?
- Do you admire them, pity them, or feel indifferent?

### Why did you agree to form a party with them?
- Are you bound by duty, friendship, necessity, or ulterior motives?
- What do you hope to gain from this alliance?

## Freeform Backstory
If, after answering all the above, you want to provide me with a narrative backstory, go for it. I always aim to have at least one arc linked to each characters backstory. If there is something in your backstory you specifically want to explore, please highlight it to me. Additionally, any key information, such as names and places, please highlight and extract from the narrative of your backstory so I can find them at a glance.
