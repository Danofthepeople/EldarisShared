# D&D is a Universal Constant
D&D runs on Saturdays at 7pm every week. I do this consistently, and if there is going to be a cancelled session you will know days or weeks in advance. Extend similar courtesy to me, tell me when you will be late, and give warning for sessions you will miss. No excuse for ghosting. If you are regularly late or miss sessions then why are you even here.

# Don't Be Cunts
By this I mean don't be cunts to each other, or to me. Keep any conflict in character, and recognise everyone is here for fun. Spoiling other peoples fun will result in being told off, or kicked.
 
# Saying No 
Sometimes you will be told no to your ideas, this is not an insult to you. It's usually me trying to preserve the laws of physics, or some key bits of worldbuilding, I'll provide my reasoning when I can. Don't bog down the session by constantly arguing a point.
 
# Share the Spotlight
There are 5 of you, that means not all of you can be talking at the same time. So play nice and share the spotlight. For my part I will actively rotate around the players, but you guys can help by being conscious that sometimes you aren't the focus. I don't need to remind three of you about the dark days, lets not return to that. If you can't share with 4 other people then this might not be the place for you.
 
# Rules
Now there are a lot of rules in this game I remember a lot of them, but I am not a machine. Help me out by learning what your shit does, it helps keep the game moving, especially if you remember things I don't. Sometimes I might make quick rulings on the fly, and check the rules at another point. In those cases I'll let you know post the session how something will be ruled in the future. In addition if you know you have some rule questions before a session, ping me at any point during the week and we can sort it out of session time.
 
# Combat Speed
Combat has the potential to crawl, be ready to take your turn before I get to you. Have a default action if you can't think.
 
# Themes
I generally cover a lot of themes, some of them can be darker than others. Some things won't be touched, you might be able to seduce the barkeep, but don't expect a sex scene. If you have anything that you explicitly don't want in the campaign, let everyone know. Also I may play some dark or horrible characters, that is in no way a reflection of who I am, I'm far worse.
 
# I'm Not Your Enemy 
Believe me I want your characters to be cool almost as much as you do, that sometimes means I must try and destroy them, it's not personal, honest.

Sometimes an in world character might call your characters ugly, this does not mean you are ugly. However, feel free to send me three angry paragraphs if I ever do it. This seriously happened. 

Anything that happens to your character should not go beyond your character.

# Player Safety
Player safety is very important to me. I want to expand the tools at my disposal to help with this, and if you have any suggestions, let me know.

Communication is eminently important when player safety is involved. If the session strays into territory you are uncomfortable with, call it out. There will never be any pushback, and boundaries will be respected. If I, or any player, crosses a previously set boundary by mistake, call it out.

To help me, please let me know any subjects that cannot be referenced in any way, any subjects that can only happen off screen, and anything that can happen but without exacting detail.

In my mind there are roughly four states:
- **All is Good** No one is uncomfortable, and play continues as usual.
- **Slow Down** We are straying close to sensitive subjects, lets take it slow and make sure everyone is okay.
- **Rewind** That was too far, lets reset to before that happened and take a different path.
- **Hard Stop** Something went too far, and a hard stop is called. The session pauses, everyone takes a break. Once everyone is ready, we roll back to before the hard stop and take a different path.

If you are ever uncomfortable, feel free to call out one of these states. If you believe more states would be helpful let me know.

# Everyone Gets a Chance
Those of you who have played with me before know this, but everyone gets a chance to explore their character. Sometimes we will be following another person's backstory for a while, remain engaged for at some point it will be your turn. This game could last for literal years, don't worry about not getting a chance, and don't try to do everything at once.
 
# Do What You Want
You don't have to do things I set out for you, but some things will happen with or without you. The world exists, your characters should have goals, feel free to follow them. On the same note however, if you decide upon a path, make me plan for it, then change your mind, quality will drop. Once you get on the ride don't pick a new one until you have finished the current rollercoaster.
 
# Help Me Plan Ahead
If you have plans and goals for your character, don't keep them secret from me. Surprisingly the sooner I know about them the better I can weave them into a plot. The last time I got information hours before a session I stole a mother's soul.
 
# Gameplay Cycle
 I like to have a balance of exploration, roleplay and combat. Expect to have opportunities to do it all.
 
# Communication
Any time there is a problem the best thing to do is tell me, anytime you want to explore something tell me, just fucking talk to me if you want something to change.
