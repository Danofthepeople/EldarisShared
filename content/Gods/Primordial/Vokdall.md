# Honorifics
The Purging Fire, The Burning One, The Ekindler, The Flame Keeper, The Inferno.

# Description
Heat and fire is vital to life, when controlled great or terrible things can be accomplished. Vokdall is the fourth of Xemos, and it is only Xemos's hand that they won't burn. Beware those who meddle with fire, for a loss of control brings indiscriminate destruction to all.

# Classification
Primordial.

# Divine Domains
Forge, Light, Nature.

# Avatar
A turtle with flesh of magma and igneous rock, instead of a shell, an active volcano stands on Vokdall’s back.

# Holy Symbols
An erupting volcano on the back of a turtle.

# Well Known Aspects
- Asondel The Crimson Blade - Aspect embodying the fire elementals and the elemental plane of fire, where relentless flames, molten lava, and searing heat exist in an ever-burning realm of pure, unyielding fire.
- Ginmik Bronzearm - Aspect embodying the untamed fury of fire, where uncontrolled flames rage, consuming everything in their path and leaving nothing but destruction in their wake.
- Emberheart- Aspect embodying the life-giving heat of fire, the essential warmth that sustains growth, fuels creation, and brings vitality to all living things.
