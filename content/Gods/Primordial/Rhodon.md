# Honorifics
The Earthen One, The Mountain, The Grand Titan, The Quakebringer, The Stone Lord.

# Description
Rhodon is the second of [[Xemos]], and the very ground we walk on, the foundation upon which our lives are permitted. The impassible mountain, the grain of sand, the stone in our walls, the gems on our fingers, Rhodon is all these things and more.

# Classification
Primordial.

# Divine Domains
Justice, Mountain, Nature, Order.

# Avatar
An Immense stone titan carrying an Island paradise on his shoulders.

# Holy Symbols
A grand titan, lifting an island.

# Well Known Aspects
- Anwid The Diamond - Aspect embodying the earth elementals and the endless expanse of the elemental plane of earth, where stone shifts, mountains walk, and the land itself breathes with ancient power.
- Minnarak Honourhorn - Aspect embodying worked stone, from towering city walls to grand castles and intricate statues, representing the mastery of mortals in shaping the earth into enduring monuments of civilization.
- Quake - Aspect embodying the destructive force of the earth, from quaking ground to yawning fissures, reminding all that even the firmest foundation can be shattered.
