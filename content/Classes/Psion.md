# Overview & Creation
Psions tap into the raw force of their own minds, bending the world around them with thought alone. No books, no bloodlines, no rituals; just pure, unfiltered will. They are subtle when they wish to be and devastating when they don't. Most folk fear Psions more than any wizard, for you cannot see the moment they decide you no longer belong in the world, you only feel your mind unravel.

## Hit Points
- **Hit Dice:** d6 per Psion level
- **Hit Points at first Level:** 6 + your Constitution modifier
- **Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d6 + your Constitution modifier per Psion level after 1st, re-rolling allowed on a 1.

## Proficiencies
- **Armour:** Light armour
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons
- **Tools:** None
- **Saving Throws:** Intelligence, Wisdom
- **Skills:** Psionics, and choose two from Deception, History, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Medicine, Perception, or Religion

## Subclasses
- **Awakened Mind:** An Awakened Mind is a psion who had their psionic power awakened within them by an encounter, event, or circumstance. Perhaps their powers were latent or perhaps their destiny had been that of a normal creature until powers were suddenly thrust on them. Perhaps they brushed the realms beyond in a vivid dream, perhaps they wandered the Feywild and came back warped, perhaps they merely glimpsed the eyes of a creature that did not belong walking down the street.
- **Unleashed Mind:** An Unleashed Mind’s power is most often an innate force they struggle to understand or control, a reflection of their state of mind and mood projected onto the world around them, often to devastating results. The life of an Unleashed Psion is frequently their journey to control their unruly powers... or one of embracing the destructive powers fully
- **Transcended Mind:** A Transcended psion is most often one that has found their path to psionic powers through a mental epiphany, realizing the place of their mind within the multiverse and how it ties to everything else, seeing the keys and levers to the world laid out before them in their mind’s eye.
- **Shaper’s Mind:** A Shaper is a Psion that specializes in the materialization of their imagination, projecting it out into the world. No mere conjurers borrowing the powers of other planes, a Shaper manifests things from nothing but their own mind, weaving their creations into existence through the exertion of raw psionic power and imagination.
- **Wandering Mind:** A Wandering Mind is among the more mysterious incarnates of psions, these are individuals that just aren’t quite rooted in the same reality everyone else is... they find it more pliable and slippery, and prone to jumping right through it on occasion.
- **Elemental Mind:** An Elemental Mind is a Psion that can manifest and control elements as an extension of their will.
- **Consuming Mind:** A Consuming Mind is a master of a dangerous branch of psionics, born from the ability to sap energy from other creatures. This power can tear away thoughts, minds, and ultimately vitality for the psion’s own consumption
