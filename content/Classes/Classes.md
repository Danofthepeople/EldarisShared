# Foreword
I have aimed to put together a comprehensive list of all available classes, subclasses, and how they fit into Eldairs. Most of this will be from the point of view of the Union, as that will be where your character is from. Some classes and subclasses may contain lore that is incompatible with Eldaris, if something in a class or subclasses lore interests you, let me know

In general I am open to homebrew, and stealing mechanics from d&d 24, so long as there is some semblance of balance. All homebrew and d&d 24 class stuff needs to be ran by me before it can be added to your character. 

There are a few items of preapproved homebrew/third party content.
- Bone Wizard's Buffed Monk: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1UDV_McpulBLVYz8msq65gIcHlyxiPHcBhcKZevw1yWQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.mgj6shf0p7c7
- KibblesTasty's Classes: https://www.kthomebrew.com/ (this does not currently extend to subclasses but may in the future)

All Esper Genesis Core Manual classes/subraces have been deliberately excluded, and thus are not available. Everything else that you can find in the roll20 compendium is yours to use, though that doesn't mean some of the stuff won't need balance tweaks.

# The Arcanum
"Power is knowledge made manifest."

## Classes
- [[Artificer]]: "Magic is a tool. Master the tool, and you master the impossible."
- [[Psion]]: "The mind shapes reality. All else is illusion."
- [[Sorcerer]]: "Power is not learned — it is born."
- [[Wizard]]: "The world obeys those who know its secret names."

## Commonfolk Perception
- "A Wizard'll promise you a spell to mend your house, and accidentally set it on fire instead."
- "Sorcerers are like storms: beautiful to watch, terrible to stand too close to."
- "Artificers can fix anything, or break it worse. Either way, you'll pay double."
- "Psions don't even need words to curse you. Blink wrong at one and you'll be dreaming of knives for a month."

## Authority Perception
- "Essential to progress, inevitable in disaster."
- "License their work, limit their reach, praise them in public, and always build a second door out of the room."
- "No war was ever won without mages. No empire ever fell without them, either."

## Rivalries
**Wizard vs Sorcerer:**
Wizard: "A Sorcerer is like a child with a fire, too much power, no understanding of it."
Sorcerer: "And a Wizard is a starving man poring over cookbooks. You chase what I already am."

**Sorcerer vs Artificer**:
Sorcerer: "All the gears and gadgets in the world won't give you a soul for magic."
Artificer: "And you are a waste of potential, living proof that power without craftsmanship is just a candle waiting to gutter out."

**Artificer vs Wizard:**
Artificer: "Your spells are delicate lacework. A few loose threads and it all collapses. I'll take solid metal and gears, thanks."
Wizard: "Gears rust. Knowledge endures. I build my legacy in the very laws of the universe."

**Psion vs Wizard:**
Psion: "You reach for power outside yourself. I simply awaken what was always within."
Wizard: "And when your mind frays and crumbles, who will reweave it? Not you."

**Psion vs Sorcerer:**
Psion: "Instinct is not strength. A beast can lash out; only a mind can rule."
Sorcerer: "Tell yourself whatever you like. While your lost in your own head, I get things done."

**Artificer vs Psion:**
Artificer: "You talk about the mind shaping the world. I prefer tools that don't vanish when you get a headache."
Psion: "And I prefer not being bound by hammer and nail. like a common blacksmith."

# The Faithful
"Our cause is higher, our will unbreakable."

## Classes
- [[Cleric]]: "Voice of the divine, hand of mercy — or judgment."
- [[Paladin]]: "Blade of faith, shield of the righteous."

## Commonfolk Perception
- "A Cleric can bless your harvest or curse your bloodline, best stay on their good side."
- "Paladins? Saints in shining armour until you break a law you didn't know existed."
- "The gods walk among us, through them. Be grateful. Or terrified."
- "A coin for the temple buys peace. A prayer from the Faithful buys protection."

## Authority Perception
- "The Faithful are useful for maintaining order, until their god's orders outweigh the crown's."
- "You don’t control Clerics and Paladins; you partner with them… or prepare for a holy war."
- "Trust the Faithful with the people's hearts. Just don't trust them with a crown."

## Rivalries
**Cleric vs Paladin:**
Cleric: "You swing your sword like it carries the gods' will. Faith is not proven by blood spilled on sacred ground."
Paladin: "And you speak of mercy while evil gathers strength. Words are not walls. Sometimes the only prayer left is a blade in hand."

**Paladin vs Cleric**:
Paladin: "When the enemy stands before you, judgment must fall like thunder. Would you have us sit in council while innocents burn?"
Cleric: "Righteousness without wisdom is just vengeance with a prettier name. Even thunder waits for the storm to gather."

**Cleric vs Cleric (doctrinal disputes):**
First Cleric: "You compromise too easily. Faith demands unwavering truth, not soothing lies."
Second Cleric:  "Faith demands we heal the broken, not drive them deeper into darkness with pride disguised as piety."

**Paladin vs Paladin (different codes, different visions of justice):**
First Paladin: "We are sworn to purge the wicked, not ponder their pleas."
Second Paladin: "Mercy is not weakness. Without it, we are not holy knights, we are but another army draped in banners and regret."

# The Vanguard
"First into battle, last to fall."

## Classes
- [[Barbarian]]: "When fury leads, nothing stands."
- [[Fighter]]: "Master of every weapon, student of none."
- [[Monk]]:  "The sharpest blade is discipline."
- [[Spellblade]]: "Where magic falters, my blade endures."
- [[Warlord]]:  "Victory is the art of command."

## Commonfolk Perception
- "You hear the tramp of boots and the clash of steel, and you know the Vanguard’s come to sort it."
- "I trust a Fighter to keep a promise and a Barbarian to keep a grudge."
- "The Monk’ll bow, break your bones, and bow again. Strange folk."
- "Warlords talk fancy, but you’ll be grateful when they get you out alive."
- "Spellblade? Like a Wizard with more guts than sense."

## Authority Perception
- "Reliable. Predictable. Put them on the front line and point them at the enemy."
- "Warlords make fine officers, Fighters make fine soldiers, and Barbarians make fine shock troops, just don’t mix the three without a leash."
- "Spellblades are useful, but keep a Mage near them. They're clever, not wise."
- "Monks are disciplined, but gods help you if they start preaching."

## Rivalries
**Fighter vs Warlord:**
Fighter: "I don't need a map or a speech to know where the enemy is, it’s in front of me."
Warlord: "Bravery without strategy is just suicide with extra steps."

**Monk vs Barbarian:**
Monk: "He who cannot master himself will never master his foe."
Barbarian: "You keep meditating. I’ll keep smashing. Let’s see who wins first."

**Spellblade vs Fighter:**
Spellblade: "A blade without magic is just another piece of iron."
Fighter: "Magic’s a crutch. Try fighting with nothing but your will and see who’s left standing."

**Warlord vs Spellblade:**
Warlord: "Fancy tricks win battles; discipline wins wars."
Spellblade: "Your wars would end faster if you knew how to end a fight, not just win it."

**Barbarian vs Warlord:**
Barbarian: "No plan survives a punch to the mouth."
Warlord: "Luckily for you, my plan does not involve you thinking."

**Monk vs Spellblade:**
Monk: "True strength lies within, not in borrowed magic."
Spellblade: "Strength is useless if it dies with you. Adapt or perish."

**Barbarian vs Fighter:**
Barbarian: "You dance around your enemy. I become their nightmare."
Fighter: "Strength without skill is a old sword waiting to shatter."

**Monk vs Warlord:**
Monk: "A mind clouded with ambition leads armies into ruin."
Warlord: "I’ll take ruin over surrender. Ambition wins kingdoms."

# The Veiled
"We walk the paths you fear to see."

## Classes
- [[Bard]]: A song, a story, a smile; choose your weapon."
- [[Occultist]]: "The old rites whisper louder than any monarch’s decree."
- [[Rogue]]: "The unseen blade cuts deepest."
- [[Warlock]]: "Power has its price,  one  I was willing to pay"

## Commonfolk Perception
- "Never trust a Warlock to keep a bargain, they only honour deals made with their patrons."
- "An Occultist’ll bless your home one day and sell your soul the next, all because the spirits told them to."
- "A Bard'll steal your heart, your purse and your lover, and you'll thank them for it."
- "Rogues smile like they know how and when you'll die. Maybe they do."

## Authority Perception
- "Necessary evils, all of them. Better to have them under watch than working for your enemies."
- "A Bard wins hearts, a Rogue wins secrets, a Warlock wins pacts, and an Occultist wins... something best not discussed."
- "Pay them well, flatter them better, and never, ever,  turn your back."
## Rivalries
**Warlock vs Occultist:**
Warlock: "You cling to dusty rites and dead gods. I struck a bargain with living power."
Occultist: "And one day that power will come to collect. Mine already belongs to me."

**Bard vs Rogue:**
Bard: "Your blade cannot woo the crowds I win with a wink and a word."
Rogue: "And yet when the lights go out, it’s the knife they remember, not the song."

**Occultist vs Bard:**
Occultist: "While you dance and dazzle, real power listens from the dark."
Bard: "You can keep your shadows. I'll take the applause, and the key to the treasury."

**Rogue vs Warlock:**
Rogue: "You sell your soul for a shortcut. I may earn my living the hard way, but I can still sleep at night."
Warlock: "Oh but I sleep wonderfully, little thief. While you are stealing purses, I am ascending to greatness."

**Occultist vs Rogue:**
Occultist: "You scurry through the dark I command, and call yourself its master, pathetic."
Rogue: "The dark may be your domain, but I make a living slipping through such places and slitting their master's throat."

**Bard vs Warlock:**
Bard: "You sell so much for power, while I can achieve greatness with simple words."
Warlock: "Your words may spin mortal kinds, but mortals have limits. My patrons does not, and neither do I."

# The Wildsoul
"We are the land's memory, its teeth, and its voice."

## Classes
- [[Druid]]: "We are the will of the wild made flesh."
- [[Ranger]]: "We walk the wild paths, and guard them with steel and arrow."
- [[Warden]]: "The land endures because we make it so."

## Commonfolk Perception
- "A Druid can make your fields bloom... or your village vanish under vines."
- "Rangers come when the beasts grow bold or the bandits grow clever. Thank the gods for them."
- "Wardens are the ones you call when the world itself gets angry — and sometimes they’re angrier than the land."

## Authority Perception
- "Wildsoul types are best left to the borders and forests. Invite them into the city, and don't be surprised if the city starts growing trees in the cobblestones."
- "Good for hunting monsters. Bad for following orders."
- "Respect them, bribe them, fear them — just don't try to own them."

## Rivalries
**Druid vs Ranger:**
Druid: "You serve the land, yet carry steel to wound it. How many trees have you felled for your arrows?"
Ranger: "Better a bow in hand than prayers in the wind. Protecting the wild means knowing how to fight for it."

**Ranger vs Warden:**
Ranger: "You bind the strength of the wilds, yet claim to be its defender?"
Warden: "The wilds deserve a champion who is bonded to it with more than just words welp."

**Warden vs Druid:**
Warden: "You speak of balance, yet let rot and predator reign as they will. The wild needs strength, not sermons."
Druid: "And you mistake domination for guardianship. True strength bends like a willow."
